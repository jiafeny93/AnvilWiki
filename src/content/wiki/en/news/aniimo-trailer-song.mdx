## How to Identify an Aniimo Trailer Song Yourself

Because the trailer music appears to be original, song-recognition apps often come up empty — and that failure is actually a useful clue rather than a dead end. The table below ranks the most reliable methods:

| Method | Best For | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| Read the YouTube description | Official credits when they exist | Descriptions for unreleased games often omit music information |
| Shazam or SoundHound | Licensed commercial tracks | Usually fails on original score written for the game |
| Ask the Aniimo community | Crowdsourced identifications | Fan answers can be wrong; verify against official sources |
| Check the official wiki | wiki.aniimo.com carries official beta-era data | Official source, but beta content may be revised after launch |
| Follow official channels | New uploads and credit updates | Music can change between beta and launch |

A few practical tips: note the exact timestamp of the section you want identified, since vocal passages are far easier to describe than ambient instrumentation. Run Shazam or SoundHound directly against the official upload rather than a re-upload, and try it during the clearest melodic moments. When asking in the community, always link the official video with your timestamp included. Finally, remember that anything sourced from beta-era footage may simply change.
