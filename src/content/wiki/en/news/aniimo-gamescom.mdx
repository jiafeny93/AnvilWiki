The next-gen creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) RPG Aniimo has closed out its Gamescom 2026 run in Cologne, and this page now stands as a full recap of the showing. Pawprint Studio's free-to-play open-world action RPG used the biggest week in European gaming to lock its launch plan in public: a musical trailer during Opening Night Live that ended on the September 16, 2026 PC and console release date (mobile follows on September 23), plus a hands-on demo on the show floor.

With the global closed beta concluded and more than 5 million pre-registrations banked, Gamescom 2026 was the final major marketing stop before launch day. Whether you missed the broadcasts or want to revisit what was shown, this recap gathers everything that happened around Aniimo at the convention — the trailers, the booth, the demo, and what it all means for September 16.

> **Last updated: September 7, 2026** — Gamescom 2026 has concluded, so this page is now a recap rather than a preview. Launch-week coverage lives on the [release date hub](/release/aniimo-release-date/).

## What Happened at Opening Night Live

Opening Night Live on August 25, hosted by Geoff Keighley, carried Aniimo's headline moment of the week. The game premiered a music-driven trailer — a full musical number rather than a standard cinematic — and closed it with the announcement players had been waiting for: Aniimo launches on September 16, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store. PlayStation Blog listed Aniimo among the show's highlights, and outlets picked the date reveal as one of the night's standout moments.

A dedicated release date trailer followed during show week, putting the September 16 date on record a second time alongside gameplay footage. That makes two official trailers from the gamescom window — the musical ONL segment and the release date cut — both now on the official [Aniimo YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Aniimo_EN).

## The Aniimo Booth in Hall 6

On the show floor itself, Aniimo ran a booth at **Hall 6, Booth 1C041**, open across the public days of **August 28–30**. Visitors could go hands-on with the latest playable demo and meet the Pawprint Studio team in person — a rare chance to question the developers directly ahead of launch. The booth also ran a Susuta custom Xbox controller giveaway, one of the more eye-catching collectibles at this year's show.

Cologne was not the team's first rodeo this year. Aniimo had already appeared at PAX East in Boston (March 26–29) with a playable demo, so the Gamescom setup followed a proven template: playable hardware, developers on hand, and the social channels posting straight from the floor.

With the date announced from the ONL stage, every remaining question is about the launch window itself rather than whether the game ships in 2026. The confirmed lineup is Steam, the Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S on September 16 — with Xbox Play Anywhere support — and iOS and Android following on September 23. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Was Aniimo at Gamescom 2026?

Yes — Aniimo attended Gamescom 2026 in Cologne this past August. The team ran a booth at Hall 6, Booth 1C041 across the public days (August 28–30) with a hands-on demo, and the game premiered a musical trailer during Opening Night Live on August 25. It was the title's second playable convention appearance of 2026 after PAX East in March.

### Did you need a ticket to play the Aniimo demo?

Yes — playing the demo on the show floor required a standard ticket to enter the convention in Cologne. The trailers and announcements, on the other hand, streamed free online, and every broadcast remains available to rewatch.

### Was the Aniimo release date announced at Gamescom 2026?

Yes — Opening Night Live delivered the date. The musical trailer ended with the announcement that Aniimo launches on September 16, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), with mobile following on September 23. A dedicated release date trailer also premiered during show week.

### What was in the Aniimo Gamescom playable demo?

The booth demo gave attendees hands-on time with a recent build of the game; the exact platform and content list were never itemized. Event builds typically run on PC or current-gen consoles to show fidelity at its best, and the full game is confirmed for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, iOS, and Android.

### Where can I rewatch the Aniimo Gamescom 2026 reveals?

The Opening Night Live 2026 replay on YouTube carries the Aniimo musical segment, and the official Aniimo YouTube channel (@Aniimo_EN) and X account (@Aniimo_EN) host the standalone trailers. The official Discord remains the best place for developer follow-ups as launch approaches.
