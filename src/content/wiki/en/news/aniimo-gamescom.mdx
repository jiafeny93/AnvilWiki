## New Creatures, Biomes, and Twine Mechanics on Display

The heart of Aniimo lies in its magical creatures and the unique Twine system. Thus far, the community has been captivated by officially revealed creatures such as the fiery Emberpup, the shadowy Darkler, and the enigmatic Eklue. However, the continent of Idyll is vast, and a massive stage like this is the perfect opportunity to expand the game's roster.

During the showcases at the event, fans are eagerly anticipating the reveal of new elemental types, evolved forms, and deeper insights into how Twine abilities synergize in real-time combat. Because Aniimo blends open-world ARPG mechanics with traditional creature-catching, the demos will likely highlight complex elemental reactions, dodging, and active skill usage rather than turn-based menu navigation.

Furthermore, fans are hoping to see more of Idyll’s diverse biomes. Previous trailers and beta tests have shown lush forests and rocky terrains, but an event of this caliber is the ideal place to show off underwater exploration, aerial traversal using bonded creatures, or harsh, snowy climates.
