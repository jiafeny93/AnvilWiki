If you are eager to dive into the next generation of creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) RPGs, you are likely looking for an invite to the Aniimo beta. Developed by Pawprint Studio and published by FunPlus, Aniimo is an upcoming free-to-play open-world action RPG that tasks players—known as Pathfinders—with exploring the vast continent of Idyll. Revealed during the 2025 Xbox Games Showcase, the game promises real-time combat, co-op [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/), and a unique "Twine" bonding system that lets you see through the eyes of your magical companions. Because the full launch is still on the horizon, beta testing is currently the only way to get hands-on experience. Here is everything you need to know about the current testing phases, how to register, and what platforms are supported.

## Aniimo Beta Phases and Schedule

As of the latest official updates, Aniimo's most recent test was the [Second Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-beta-2/) in January 2026 — there is no active public test running right now. That test stressed server capacities, exercised the real-time action combat mechanics, and gathered player feedback on the early-game open-world progression across the continent of Idyll. The next fixed milestone is the global launch on September 16, 2026. 

Pawprint Studio has been relatively methodical with its rollout. Unlike open betas that allow anyone to download and play freely, the current Aniimo beta is strictly limited to invited participants and selected content creators. These tests are frequently under Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), meaning gameplay footage or screenshots may not always be publicly shareable, though the studio occasionally lifts these restrictions for promotional events.

The developers are expected to showcase new playable content, expanded creature rosters, and potentially announce the next major public testing wave during Gamescom 2026 in Cologne this August. Players should keep a close eye on official channels during major industry events for immediate code drops or open registration windows.

## How to Sign Up for the Aniimo Beta

Getting into the Aniimo beta requires a bit of proactive effort. Because the game has already amassed over 5 million pre-registrations globally, competition for limited closed beta slots is incredibly high. Here is the step-by-step process to maximize your chances of securing access:

1. **Official Website Registration:** Visit the official Aniimo website. Log in or create a FunPlus account and officially pre-register. This is the baseline requirement for being considered for any email-based beta invites.
2. **Join the Official Discord:** Community interaction is heavily rewarded in modern game testing. Join the official Aniimo Discord server. Developers frequently use Discord to announce surprise playtest drops, host beta-key giveaways, and interact directly with the community.
3. **Follow Social Channels:** Keep notifications on for the official Aniimo YouTube, X (Twitter), and Instagram accounts. Pawprint Studio occasionally runs community contests where active and lucky followers win closed beta access.
4. **Link Your Platform Accounts:** Ensure your FunPlus account is correctly linked to your intended gaming platform (Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation, Xbox, or Mobile). An incomplete profile can disqualify you from automated beta selection.

| Discord Community | Join, verify, and participate in events | Medium |

## Platforms Supported in the Aniimo Beta

While Aniimo is confirmed to be launching across a wide array of platforms, beta tests usually begin on specific ecosystems to streamline technical troubleshooting. During past closed beta tests — including the [Second Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-beta-2/) in January 2026 — testing focused primarily on PC to verify core server architecture, gameplay mechanics, and graphical fidelity, with Android tests delivered via the Aniimo Beta test app on Google Play.

For the final release, Aniimo will be available on Steam and the Epic Games Store for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S (featuring Xbox Play Anywhere), and Mobile devices (iOS and Android). It is highly anticipated that console and mobile beta tests will follow as the full 2026 launch approaches, ensuring the cross-platform functionality and mobile optimizations are up to standard before release.

If you are primarily a console or mobile player, you should still pre-register now. Developers often prioritize players who registered early when rolling out tests for new platforms down the line.

## What to Expect: Gameplay and Features

For those lucky enough to access the Aniimo beta, the core experience revolves around exploring the vibrant world of Idyll and utilizing the "Twine" system. Unlike traditional turn-based creature catchers, Aniimo leans heavily into open-world action RPG mechanics. You play as a Pathfinder, engaging in real-time combat alongside your bonded magical creatures.

The Twine system is the standout feature being tested. It allows players to form a deep, mystical bond with creatures known as Aniimo. Through this connection, Pathfinders can essentially see through the eyes of their Aniimo and wield their unique abilities dynamically in combat and traversal. 

During the beta, players will likely encounter several creatures already teased in official showcases, such as the fiery Emberpup or other recognizable companions. Specific stats, hard [evolution](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/) lines, and end-game creature rosters remain under wraps and are actively being balanced based on beta tester feedback.

| Multiplayer / Co-op | Teaming up for fantasy quests and combat | Limited stress testing |
| Creature Roster | Bonding, leveling, and combat roles | Subject to change and NDA |

## Will There Be an Open Beta Before Launch?

Given that Aniimo is officially slated for a full release in 2026, an Open Beta or a broader Public Test Realm (PTR) is highly likely. Developers typically use closed tests for bug fixing and balance changes, followed by an open test to stress-test server capacities before the global launch.

With over 5 million players already pre-registered, a sudden server influx on launch day is a major concern for FunPlus. An Open Aniimo beta would allow the team to simulate launch-day traffic. While no specific dates for an Open Beta have been officially confirmed, industry expectations point toward a wider playtest occurring shortly after Gamescom 2026, assuming the current closed testing phases proceed smoothly. Keep your account verified and stay tuned to official newsletters for the latest invitations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Aniimo beta free to play?

Yes, the Aniimo beta is completely free to access if you are selected. Aniimo is a free-to-play game, and developers do not charge players to participate in closed or open testing phases.

### Can I play the Aniimo beta on mobile or console?

Past Aniimo closed beta tests were primarily PC-focused, with mobile tests distributed through the Aniimo Beta test app on Google Play. The full game launches on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile devices alongside PC on September 16, 2026.

### How do I know if I got selected for the Aniimo beta?

If you are selected, you will typically receive an email from FunPlus or Pawprint Studio to the address associated with your pre-registration. You may also receive a direct message or a redeemable key code through the official Aniimo Discord server.

### Will my progress in the Aniimo beta carry over to the full release?

No, beta progress for Aniimo is not expected to carry over to the live game. Beta tests are strictly for testing and balancing, so the servers will be wiped before the official global launch on September 16, 2026.

### Is there an NDA for the Aniimo beta?

Certain phases of the Aniimo closed beta are expected to be under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), meaning you cannot stream or post gameplay. However, the developers occasionally lift the NDA for specific promotional playtests, so always check your invite email for the current rules.

### When is the exact release date for Aniimo?

Aniimo's global [release date](/release/aniimo-release-date/) is confirmed for September 16, 2026. The game is in final pre-launch preparation after multiple closed beta tests.
