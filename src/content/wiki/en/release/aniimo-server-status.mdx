Searching for Aniimo server status right now gets you a short answer: **the servers are not online yet.** Aniimo [launches globally](/release/aniimo-release-date/) on September 16, 2026, and until that day there are no live servers to check. This page is the status hub for launch week — what goes wrong on big launch days, how to tell an outage from a problem on your end, and where an outage gets officially confirmed fastest.

When September 16 arrives and something feels broken, work down this list in order. It separates "everyone is affected" from "it's just you" in under two minutes:

## Before Launch — Why Nothing Connects Yet

Every "server unreachable," "cannot connect," or timeout before September 16 has one cause: **the servers simply are not live.** The final [closed beta test](/release/aniimo-global-closed-beta/) ended in July, beta servers were shut down with it, and no early-access or head-start window has been announced. Anyone offering you a way to "play early" through a mirror client, private server, or APK modification is either selling malware or a scam — the legitimate route in is the [global launch](/release/aniimo-release-date/) on September 16, with [pre-registration](/release/aniimo-pre-register/) rewards waiting for those who signed up.

## What Launch Day Will Look Like

Aniimo is a [free-to-play](/release/aniimo-free-to-play/) title with [cross-platform multiplayer](/release/aniimo-platforms/) across PC, consoles, and mobile — which means millions of players can hit the servers in the same hours. Based on how launches of this scale behave, expect:

- **Login queues and slow queue movement** in the first hours. This is capacity, not breakage.
- **Timeouts at the login handshake.** Retrying rapidly makes it worse; spaced retries or waiting 10-15 minutes usually clears it.
- **A day-one patch** on top of the pre-load. If the update stalls, that is download congestion — the [download fix section](/release/aniimo-error-codes/) covers it.
- **Staged rollouts or login waves**, if the publisher uses them. A locked "servers not yet open" message with staggered regional times is a sign of control, not failure.

The good news from the beta program: Pawprint Studio ran multiple rounds of global closed tests specifically to pressure-test server load and the login pipeline. The bad news no beta fully replicates is launch-day scale — every studio gets a rude first hour, and what matters is how fast status communication runs.

## Outage or Local Problem — Telling Them Apart

The single most useful habit: **check whether other people are affected before touching your own settings.** If the Discord and social channels are quiet and players in community channels are playing normally, work the local checklist instead — restart the client fully, sign out and back into your platform account, and rule out VPN or proxy interference, which is the top cause of false "cannot connect" errors. The complete per-symptom walkthrough lives in the [error-code guide](/release/aniimo-error-codes/).

One launch-week trap to skip: do not reinstall the game as a first move. A fresh 30-plus-GB download during congestion usually makes things worse, and almost no launch-day problem is actually fixed by it. Storage and file-verification steps are covered in the error-code guide when they are genuinely the right tool.

## After the First 48 Hours

Launch congestion flattens within a day or two, and the status picture switches to the normal live-service rhythm: announced maintenance windows (usually with compensation for downtime), emergency hotfixes, and occasional regional incidents. From launch week onward, this page stays the quick triage point — symptom tables, the fastest official confirmation channels, and links into the specific fix guides — while the [multiplayer guide](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) covers everything that is working-as-intended.
