If you are planning your setup for the upcoming next-gen creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) adventure, you are likely wondering about the available Aniimo platforms. Developed by Pawprint Studio and published by FunPlus, Aniimo is an ambitious open-world action RPG that was officially revealed during the 2025 Xbox Games Showcase. The game is making its way to a remarkably wide array of systems, ensuring that Pathfinders can explore the magical continent of Idyll almost anywhere. From PC storefronts to current-generation consoles and mobile devices, the cross-platform strategy is a major focus for the developers. In this comprehensive guide, we break down exactly which systems are getting the game, what we know about cross-play and cross-progression, and how the mobile version compares to its console counterparts.

*Note: Because Aniimo is currently in its Global Closed Beta and approaching its full 2026 launch, specific details regarding native resolutions, frame rates, and final mobile requirements are subject to change. Below is a breakdown of confirmed facts versus current expectations based on official showcases.*

## All Confirmed Aniimo Platforms Explained

Pawprint Studio has confirmed that Aniimo will be a multi-platform release from day one. As a free-to-play title, the developers are aiming for maximum accessibility across the Western and Eastern gaming markets. 

Here is the breakdown of the confirmed platforms:

- **PC (Windows):** Confirmed for Steam and the Epic Games Store.
- **PlayStation 5:** Current-generation Sony console.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Current-generation Microsoft consoles (supporting Xbox Play Anywhere).
- **Mobile (iOS & Android):** Smartphones and tablets.

| Platform Category | Specific Platforms | Confirmation Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PC | Steam, Epic Games Store | Confirmed via official store pages |
| Console | PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S | Confirmed at 2025 Xbox Games Showcase |
| Mobile | iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play) | Confirmed for full 2026 launch |
| Legacy / Last-Gen | PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch | NOT planned / No current plans |

If you are searching for Aniimo platforms to see if your preferred device is supported, you can rest easy knowing the game is skipping last-generation hardware (PS4, Xbox One) to fully leverage next-gen open-world capabilities and real-time combat mechanics.

## Console Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S Experience

Aniimo was prominently featured during an Xbox showcase, heavily implying a strong partnership with Microsoft. However, it is also launching day-and-date on PlayStation 5. 

### What to Expect on Consoles
Because the game is entirely skipping the PS4 and Xbox One, the developers can push the boundaries of the game's "Twine" bonding system and large-scale fantasy quests. Players can expect:

- **Fast Load Times:** Leveraging the solid-state drives (SSDs) of the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S for near-instant travel across Idyll.
- **High Visual Fidelity:** 4K resolution targets with dynamic lighting to accentuate the magical creatures.
- **Xbox Play Anywhere:** This feature is expected to be supported, meaning if you buy digital content or unlock achievements on Xbox, they should sync with the PC version of the game.

For PlayStation players, DualSense haptic feedback integration is highly anticipated but has not been explicitly detailed in official gameplay drops yet. 

## Aniimo Mobile: iOS and Android Details

Perhaps the most exciting inclusion among the Aniimo platforms is mobile. Bringing a massive open-world action RPG to iOS and Android is a technical feat, and it allows players to take their Aniimo companions on the go.

### Mobile Cross-Play and Performance
The mobile version will reportedly be the exact same game as the PC and console versions, allowing for seamless [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) co-op. You will be able to team up with friends playing on high-end PCs right from your smartphone.

Because the game is currently in Global Closed Beta, the full list of supported iOS and Android devices is TBA. However, based on the visual fidelity shown in early trailers, it is safe to expect that the game will require relatively recent flagship devices (e.g., newer iPhone models and high-tier Android phones) to run smoothly at acceptable frame rates.

## Will Aniimo Support Cross-Play and Cross-Progression?

Given the wide array of Aniimo platforms, a unified account system is crucial. FunPlus and Pawprint Studio have heavily emphasized a connected, co-op fantasy experience. 

While the developers have stated that multiplayer fantasy quests and real-time combat are core to the experience, the exact backend infrastructure for cross-progression is still being finalized. Historically, free-to-play games of this magnitude utilize a central publisher account (in this case, an Aniimo or FunPlus account) to tether your data. 

**What is Expected:**
- **Cross-Play:** Players on Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, and Mobile should be able to seamlessly explore Idyll together.
- **Cross-Progression:** Your roster of bonded Aniimo creatures and Pathfinder progression should transfer seamlessly between devices (e.g., playing on PC at home, then continuing your adventure on mobile while commuting).

Make sure to register for an official account on the website to ensure your beta progress and pre-registration rewards are properly linked ahead of the 2026 launch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Aniimo coming to PC and Steam?

Yes, Aniimo is confirmed for PC and will be available on both Steam and the Epic Games Store. You can already wishlist the game on Steam to stay updated on its release.

### Will Aniimo be available on mobile devices (iOS and Android)?

Yes, Aniimo is officially launching on iOS and Android mobile devices. The mobile version is expected to feature cross-play, allowing you to team up with players on PC and consoles.

### Is Aniimo releasing on PlayStation and Xbox?

Aniimo is confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It will not be releasing on last-generation consoles like the PS4 or Xbox One to ensure the game meets next-gen open-world standards.

### Will Aniimo feature cross-play and cross-progression across platforms?

While full technical details are still being finalized, Aniimo is expected to support full cross-play and cross-progression. Players will likely be able to share their Pathfinder progress and creature roster across PC, console, and mobile.

### Is Aniimo on the Nintendo Switch?

No, Aniimo has not been announced for the Nintendo Switch. The developers are currently focusing on PC, current-gen consoles (PS5/Xbox Series X|S), and mobile platforms.

### Do I need a powerful PC to run Aniimo?

Exact hardware specifications have not yet been released, but because Aniimo is a next-gen open-world action RPG, you will likely need a moderately powerful PC to run it smoothly. Official PC requirements will be revealed closer to launch.
