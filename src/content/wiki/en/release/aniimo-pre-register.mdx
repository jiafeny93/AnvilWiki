Yes, Aniimo is officially confirmed to be a free-to-play game. You can download and play the full base game on PC, console, and mobile without purchasing a copy when it launches in 2026.

### Can I pre-register for Aniimo on mobile?

Yes, you can sign up for mobile updates (iOS and Android) directly through the official Aniimo website. While the exact app store pages are expected to go live closer to release, registering on the site ensures you get notified the moment they drop.

### Does pre-registering guarantee Closed Beta access?

No, pre-registering does not guarantee Closed Beta access, but it does put you in the pool of potential invitees. Beta testers are usually selected based on platform preferences, hardware specs, and regional server needs during the ongoing testing phases.

### What is the official release date for Aniimo?

The official [release date](/release/aniimo-release-date/) for Aniimo is confirmed as September 16, 2026. The developers wrapped up closed beta testing earlier this year, and pre-registration rewards carry over to the global launch.

### What platforms will Aniimo be available on?

Aniimo will be available on Steam and the Epic Games Store for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S (supporting Xbox Play Anywhere), and mobile devices (iOS and Android). It will not be available on older generation consoles.

### Do I need to pay to unlock pre-registration rewards?

No, all pre-registration milestone rewards are completely free for players who sign up before launch. Just ensure you use the same account email or platform login when you first boot up the game to claim your items.
