If you are planning to play Aniimo on PC, you are looking at one of the most anticipated creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) open-world RPGs heading to computers. Developed by Pawprint Studio and published by FunPlus, this next-gen monster-taming adventure is set to launch on Steam and the Epic Games Store. From its debut at the 2025 Xbox Games Showcase through its concluded closed beta tests, fans are eagerly waiting to explore the continent of Idyll as Pathfinders. But when exactly will Aniimo PC be available to download, and what hardware will you need? This comprehensive guide breaks down everything currently confirmed about the PC version, from expected release timing and platform availability to store pages, editions, and possible system requirements.

## How to Download and Play Aniimo on PC

Currently, Aniimo is officially confirmed for two PC storefronts: **Steam** and the **Epic Games Store**. You can already visit the official Aniimo Steam store page to add the game to your wishlist, which is the best way to stay notified about beta invites and upcoming release announcements. Pre-registration is also open globally, having already surpassed 5 million registered users. 

To get access to the game on PC as early as possible, players should ensure they have an active account on either platform and join the official Aniimo Discord server. The developer frequently drops closed beta test invitations and patch updates there. When the full launch goes live, downloading Aniimo PC will simply require searching for the title on your preferred launcher and installing it, as the game will be free-to-play.

## Aniimo PC Release Date and Beta Schedule

The official global release date for Aniimo is **September 16, 2026**. The game has finished its closed beta testing, in which select players who pre-registered tested the early builds. Fans hoping to jump in on day one can mark their calendars, and Pawprint Studio and FunPlus are still expected at Gamescom 2026 in Cologne this August with new previews ahead of launch.

### Confirmed vs. Expected Timelines

It is highly important to distinguish between what is officially locked in and what is simply speculated by the community regarding the Aniimo PC launch:

- **Confirmed:** The global launch date is September 16, 2026. Closed beta testing has concluded.
- **Rumored/Expected:** Some community members speculate an early Q4 2026 launch based on the beta schedule and Gamescom presence, but no official month or day has been locked in.
- **Not Confirmed:** Specific pre-order bonus timelines, early access periods, or open beta dates.

## Cross-Platform Play and PC Features

One of the biggest draws of playing Aniimo PC is the likelihood of seamless cross-platform [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/). The game is built around cooperative fantasy quests and real-time action combat, and publisher FunPlus has emphasized a unified ecosystem across all available platforms. 

Here is what players can expect from the PC version's feature set:

- **Cross-Progression:** Because Aniimo spans PC, consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S), and mobile, account progression is highly expected to carry over. You will likely be able to bond with your Aniimo creatures on a high-end PC and continue your journey on mobile while on the go.
- **Enhanced Graphics:** Playing on PC will allow players to push frame rates beyond 60fps at 1440p or 4K resolutions, assuming their hardware can handle it. Keyboard and mouse controls, alongside full controller support, are expected to be native.
- **No Roblox:** A common point of confusion for new players is whether Aniimo is a Roblox experience. Aniimo is a standalone game built on professional game engines by Pawprint Studio—it is **not** on Roblox.

## Monetization, Editions, and Pricing on PC

As officially confirmed by the developers, Aniimo will be a **free-to-play** title on all platforms, including the Steam and Epic Games Store PC versions. You will not need to purchase a base game to download and start your adventure as a Pathfinder in Idyll.

While exact monetization details are still under wraps and subject to change, players can expect a standard, ethical free-to-play model. This typically means cosmetic microtransactions, potential battle passes, and possibly a gacha-style system for acquiring certain creatures or specialized gear. However, Pawprint Studio has yet to reveal the exact pricing structure for in-game purchases or whether there will be special "Founder's Packs" available for PC players prior to the 2026 launch.
