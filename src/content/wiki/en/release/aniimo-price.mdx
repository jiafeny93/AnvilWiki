## Expected Monetization and the Aniimo Price Model

While the base game is free, maintaining live-service servers and developing ongoing content requires revenue. Based on the genre, the developer's statements, and the current beta tests, players can expect the Aniimo price model to rely on optional in-game purchases rather than paid expansions.

FunPlus is a veteran publisher in the free-to-play space, so it is highly likely that Aniimo will feature a familiar, fair monetization loop. Here is what players should reasonably expect:

**1. Battle Pass / Season Pass:**
A tiered progression system is practically guaranteed. Players will likely earn free rewards just by playing, but a premium track will offer cosmetics, currency, and convenience items.

**2. In-Game Currency:**
Real-world money will almost certainly be converted into a premium in-game currency (often called Crystals, Gems, or Coins). This currency will likely be used for cosmetic items, emotes, or speeding up timers.

**3. Cosmetic Store:**
Players can expect outfits, weapon skins, and unique visual flair for their Aniimo creatures. Crucially, monetization should be strictly kept away from pay-to-win (P2W) mechanics.

*   **Mobile (iOS/Android):** Mobile storefronts have their own localized pricing tiers. Given that Aniimo is a next-gen open-world ARPG, players on lower-end mobile devices shouldn't worry—the core game remains free, though you may be tempted by mobile-exclusive top-up offers.
