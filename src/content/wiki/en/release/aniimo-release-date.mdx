Rather than rushing the launch, Pawprint Studio appears to be taking a careful, community-driven approach. While we wait for the finalized Aniimo release date, players can look forward to seeing the game at major industry events. Aniimo has been confirmed to attend Gamescom 2026 in Cologne this August, where fans expect new trailers and potentially a narrower release window.

## Ongoing Beta Tests and Pre-Registration

Because the exact Aniimo release date is still months away, the best way to play the game right now is through the ongoing testing phases. Aniimo is currently in a **Global Closed Beta**, and the developers have opened up global pre-registration.

At the time of writing, pre-registration has already surpassed the impressive milestone of 5 million registered players. Participating in the beta not only gives you early access to the continent of Idyll but also allows you to test the game's core "Twine" bonding system—a unique mechanic that lets Pathfinders see through the eyes of their bonded creatures and wield their abilities in real-time combat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the exact Aniimo release date?

The exact Aniimo release date has not yet been announced, but the game is officially scheduled for a global launch sometime in 2026. We expect a specific date to be revealed closer to Gamescom in August 2026.

### Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes, Aniimo will be a free-to-play game on all launch platforms. While specific monetization details like battle passes or cosmetics are still under wraps, there is no cost to download and play the base game.

### What platforms will Aniimo be available on?

Aniimo will launch on Steam and the Epic Games Store for PC, as well as PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and mobile devices (iOS and Android). It is not a Roblox game.

### Can I still sign up for the Aniimo Closed Beta?

Yes, the game is currently undergoing a Global Closed Beta, and you can still sign up. To increase your chances of getting an invite, pre-register on the official website and join the official Aniimo Discord server.

### What is the Aniimo pre-registration reward?

Aniimo has surpassed 5 million global pre-registrations, but the exact milestone rewards have not been fully detailed yet. Players who pre-register on the website or wishlist the game on Steam are expected to receive exclusive launch bonuses.

### Will Aniimo have multiplayer or co-op?

Yes, Aniimo is designed as an open-world action RPG that features co-op and [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer) fantasy quests. You will be able to team up with friends to explore the continent of Idyll and battle alongside your bonded creatures.
