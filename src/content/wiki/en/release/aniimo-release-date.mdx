## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the exact Aniimo release date?

Aniimo's global release date is confirmed for September 16, 2026 on PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile (iOS and Android).

### Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes, Aniimo will be a free-to-play game on all launch platforms. While specific monetization details like battle passes or cosmetics are still under wraps, there is no cost to download and play the base game.

### What platforms will Aniimo be available on?

Aniimo will launch on Steam and the Epic Games Store for PC, as well as PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile devices (iOS and Android). It is not a Roblox game.

### Can I still sign up for the Aniimo Closed Beta?

Aniimo has finished its closed beta tests — most recently the Second Closed Beta in January 2026. The next fixed date is the global launch on September 16, 2026, so pre-register on the official website and join the official Aniimo Discord server for any future test news.

### What is the Aniimo pre-registration reward?

Aniimo has surpassed 5 million global pre-registrations, but the exact milestone rewards have not been fully detailed yet. Players who pre-register on the website or wishlist the game on Steam are expected to receive exclusive launch bonuses.

### Will Aniimo have multiplayer or co-op?

Yes, Aniimo is designed as an open-world action RPG that features co-op and [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) fantasy quests. You will be able to team up with friends to explore the continent of Idyll and battle alongside your bonded creatures.
