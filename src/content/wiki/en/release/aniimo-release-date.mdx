## Confirmed Aniimo Release Date and Timeline

The launch shipped as a staggered, two-wave release: **September 16, 2026 for PC and consoles** (Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S) and **September 23, 2026 for mobile** (iOS and Android). Revealed to the world during the 2025 Xbox Games Showcase, Aniimo (sometimes referred to by its community name, 阿尼莫) quickly became one of the most talked-about launches of the year.

### Exact Unlock Times

The official PlayStation store listing pins the PC and console unlock at **10:00 AM UTC+8 on September 16, 2026** — that is 2:00 AM UTC, or the evening of September 15 in the Americas (6:00 PM ET / 3:00 PM PT). Server regions usually open in waves around that moment; our [pre-load and unlock time guide](/release/aniimo-preload/) keeps the per-platform timetable, and the mobile unlock hour on September 23 was not separately announced.

Rather than rushing the launch, Pawprint Studio appears to be taking a careful, community-driven approach. The team gathered player feedback from its closed beta tests to polish the game's real-time combat and open-world mechanics. With launch day behind us, the [server status](/release/aniimo-server-status/) page stays worth bookmarking — launch-window queues and login errors were the norm for the first 48 hours, and that hub separates official outages from local network issues in minutes.

**Confirmed Facts:**
- PC & consoles: September 16, 2026 — official unlock 10:00 UTC+8 (02:00 UTC)
- Mobile (iOS/Android): September 23, 2026 — live since that morning
- Platforms: PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Mobile
- Official reveal: 2025 Xbox Games Showcase

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the exact Aniimo release date?

Aniimo launched on September 16, 2026 on PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with mobile (iOS and Android) following on September 23, 2026.

### Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes, Aniimo is free to download and play on all launch platforms, with optional monetization — a battle pass, a cosmetic shop, and Glimmer — layered on top of the base game.

### What platforms will Aniimo be available on?

Aniimo launched on Steam and the Epic Games Store for PC, as well as PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on September 16, 2026, with mobile devices (iOS and Android) following on September 23, 2026. It is not a Roblox game.

### Can I still sign up for the Aniimo Closed Beta?

Aniimo's closed beta tests are finished — most recently the July 2026 Global Closed Beta — and the game is now live since September 16, 2026. There is no beta to join — download the free client on your platform and start playing.

### What is the Aniimo pre-registration reward?

Aniimo surpassed 5 million global pre-registrations before launch. If you pre-registered, check your in-game mailbox and account for launch rewards — and see the codes page for the launch gift codes everyone can redeem.

### When does Aniimo come out?

Aniimo came out on September 16, 2026 for PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, and on September 23, 2026 for mobile (iOS and Android).

### Will Aniimo be on PS5?

Yes — Aniimo launched on PlayStation 5 on day one, September 16, 2026, as a free-to-play title. PS5 is one of the launch platforms alongside PC and Xbox Series X|S.

### Will Aniimo have multiplayer or co-op?

Yes, Aniimo is an open-world action RPG that features co-op and [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) fantasy quests. You can team up with friends to explore the continent of Idyll and battle alongside your bonded creatures.
