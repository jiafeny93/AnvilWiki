Aniimo Beta 2 was the second major closed test for Aniimo, the free-to-play creature-taming ARPG developed by Pawprint Studio and published by FunPlus. ## How Aniimo Beta 2 Access Worked (and How to Catch Future Tests)

Access to the second closed beta was handled through official channels rather than public sign-up pages on storefronts. On Android, testers enrolled through the **"Aniimo Beta" test app on Google Play**, which is the confirmed distribution method for mobile test phases. Invitations and key drops were announced through Aniimo's official Discord and social accounts, and players who had participated in earlier tests or actively engaged with official communities were generally seen as more likely to receive invites — though FunPlus and Pawprint Studio never published exact selection criteria.

If you're hoping to access any future test or the launch build, the practical checklist is simple:

1. **Join the official Discord** (discord.gg/aniimo) — test announcements, keys, and feedback surveys are posted there first.
2. **Pre-register** on the official site (aniimo.com) and on your platform of choice — our [pre-registration guide](/release/aniimo-pre-register/) walks through every store; global pre-registration has already passed 5 million sign-ups.
3. **Keep the Google Play beta app on your radar** if you're an Android player, and enable notifications for the store listing.
4. **Follow @Aniimo_EN on X, YouTube, and Instagram** for showcase drops and test windows.

Note that no third-party site can grant beta access — any "free Aniimo Beta 2 key" offer you see elsewhere should be treated as a scam.

## New Systems Tested in Aniimo Beta 2

The second closed beta was more than a stress test — it introduced several mechanics that shaped how the community talks about Aniimo today. Based on official reveals and beta-era documentation, these are the headline systems:

| System | What it does | Beta-only notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prismana variants | Rare, rainbow-style color variants of Aniimo that can appear during special "Prismana Flow" weather | Could be lured toward the player; variant colors were rerollable using the Shimmering Reverie item from the Lumin Crystal Shop |
| Inherit attribute system | Companion Aniimo inherit a share of the Pathfinder's character stats — such as crit rate, HP, and defense | Exact inheritance percentages seen in beta are expected to be rebalanced |
| Promise events | Limited-time beta events: Prismana Promise, Bound Promise, and Reunion Promise | Whether these return at launch in the same form is TBA |

The [Prismana system](/guides/aniimo-prismana/) is Aniimo's take on "shiny" hunting, and the beta showed a more active loop than simply re-rolling encounters: players could influence spawns during Prismana Flow weather and then re-customize colors afterward via the Lumin Crystal Shop. The [Inherit system](/guides/aniimo-inherit-system/), meanwhile, ties your character build to your companions' performance in real-time combat — a deliberate blend of ARPG progression and creature-collector design.

Treat all item names, shop currencies, and event rewards above as **beta-era details**. The Lumin Crystal Shop economy, the availability of Shimmering Reverie, and event structures may look different in the launch build.

## Does Aniimo Beta 2 Progress Carry Over?

No official statement has promised that Aniimo Beta 2 progress — captured Aniimo, levels, items, or event rewards — will carry over to the live game. As is standard for closed betas, a full progress wipe is the safe expectation, and beta accounts should be viewed as disposable test environments rather than head starts.

The same applies to any tester-exclusive rewards: unless Pawprint Studio or FunPlus explicitly announces a beta-participation reward for launch, assume nothing transfers. When in doubt, check the official Discord or wiki.aniimo.com, which remain the sources of truth.
