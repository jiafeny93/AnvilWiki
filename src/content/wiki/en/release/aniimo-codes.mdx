## What Aniimo Codes Give

The launch codes reward four kinds of items, all useful in the first hours of your expedition through [Idyll](/release/aniimo-free-to-play/):

- **Glimmer** — the premium currency that feeds the [gacha](/release/aniimo-gacha/) and cosmetic economy. Codes give it out in small amounts, but every free Glimmer is Glimmer you did not pay for.
- **Credits** — the common currency for everyday upgrades and purchases. `ANIIMOGIFT` alone hands out 20,000, and four more codes add 10,000 each.
- **Growth Flowers** — XP items that level your Aniimo faster. Stacking ten from `ANIIMOGIFT` gives a new creature an immediate head start.
- **Aniipod and Aniipod Pro** — consumable items; the Pro tier is the rarer of the two.

What codes do **not** give, as of this update: exclusive catchable creatures. Every creature in the table above's rewards is a consumable or currency — the [Twine bonding system](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) stays catch-only, which is the design line the studio has held through launch.

## How to Redeem Codes in Aniimo

Redemption is in-game on every platform. You need to finish the early tutorial area first — the pause menu is locked until then.

1. Launch Aniimo and play until the **pause menu** unlocks (end of the tutorial area).
2. Open the pause menu and click the **gear icon** (Settings).
3. Select the **Account** tab.
4. Choose **Gift Code Redemption**.
5. Enter or paste a code from the table above — codes are case-sensitive.
6. Click **Redeem**. Rewards land directly in your **inventory** — no mailbox step.

If a code is rejected, check three things in order: spelling and case, tutorial completion, and whether the code is one you already redeemed — each code works once per account. If redemption itself errors out or the game will not connect, rule out server trouble first via the [server status](/release/aniimo-server-status/) page, and the [error-code fix list](/release/aniimo-error-codes/) covers the rest.

## Squad Codes Are a Different System

The `aniimotogether` gift code is not the same thing as an Aniimo Together **squad code**, and the two are easy to confuse. Squad codes are six-character invitations used on the Aniimo Together web event: enter one to join another player's squad, or share yours to fill your own. Each squad holds up to six players, every additional member raises the squad's shared **Warehouse capacity**, and filling all six slots grants every member a bonus Aniipod. If a site publishes a random six-character string as a "gift code," it is a squad invite — the real gift codes are the strings in the table above.

The fake-code pattern to recognize: sites that demand you log in with your game account to "redeem," download a file, or complete an offer survey before showing the code. No official redemption ever works that way — the real flow is the six steps in the previous section, entirely inside the game client. If a code asks for your password, it is a scam — and if you have entered your credentials on one, changing your password immediately is step one; the [error-code fix list](/release/aniimo-error-codes/) covers account-security cleanup.
