Asking how to refund Aniimo? Here is the good news: **Aniimo is a free-to-play game, so the game itself costs nothing to download and there is no purchase to refund.** Refund questions for this title are really about optional spending — mobile in-app purchases, or any packs, battle pass tiers, or cosmetic bundles FunPlus introduces after the game launches. This guide explains what you can (and cannot) get back, platform by platform, and how to handle accidental or unauthorized charges.

> **Last updated: September 2, 2026** — Aniimo launches on PC and consoles on September 16, 2026, with mobile on September 23. No paid packs or battle pass pricing has been announced yet; refund rules below reflect each store's current published policies.

## First: The Base Game Is Free, So There Is Nothing to Refund

Every confirmed Aniimo platform — Steam, the Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, iOS, and Android — gets the game as a free download. Before launch, the only actions available are wishlisting and pre-registration, both of which are free and create no charge. That means:

- **Pre-launch:** There is nothing to refund. No pre-order, no founder pack, no charge has happened. If any website asks for payment to "reserve" Aniimo, it is a scam — the real game is free.
- **After launch:** Spending is optional and store-specific. Battle pass details and monetization have not been fully announced — see the [free-to-play](/release/aniimo-free-to-play/) overview — so your refund rights depend entirely on which store processed the payment.

If you were charged unexpectedly before the game even launches, skip straight to the unauthorized-purchase section below.

## Aniimo Refund Rules by Platform

Once paid content exists, these are the standing policies of each storefront as of the latest published rules. Always check the store's own policy page for the current wording before requesting:

| Platform | Refund window | Key conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Steam** | 14 days from purchase | Under 2 hours of relevant playtime; refunds via help.steampowered.com. Works for DLC-style purchases such as packs |
| **Epic Games Store** | 14 days from purchase | Under 2 hours of playtime; self-service in the Epic launcher's account settings |
| **PlayStation Store** | 14 days from purchase | Purchases can generally be canceled if the download has not started; add-on content is typically non-refundable once purchased |
| **Xbox (Microsoft Store)** | 14 days from purchase | Digital items must be unused or not significantly consumed; request via the Microsoft account's order history |
| **Apple App Store** | Decided case by case | Request via reportaproblem.apple.com, typically within 90 days; consumed in-game items are often refused |
| **Google Play** | 48 hours | Near-automatic refunds within 48 hours of purchase; after that, case-by-case through Google Play support |

Two platform-specific notes worth knowing. Steam's 2-hour rule measures the game's playtime, so a small cosmetic purchase does not give you 2 free hours of refund leverage on a game you have sunk 100 hours into — repeated refund abuse can also get an account flagged. And on PlayStation, the download rule is strict: once a purchase starts downloading, treat the money as likely gone unless support makes an exception.

## Refunding In-App Purchases on Mobile (iOS and Android)

Mobile is where most Aniimo refund questions will land, because that is where live-service spending concentrates. The process on each store:

**iPhone and iPad (App Store):**
1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
2. Find the Aniimo purchase, tap Report, and choose a reason such as "I didn't authorize this charge."
3. Apple responds by email, usually within 24-48 hours, and issues refunds to the original payment method when approved.

**Android (Google Play):**
1. Within 48 hours of the purchase, use the Refund option in the Google Play order history — this is close to automatic.
2. After 48 hours, request a refund through Google Play's support flow for the specific order.
3. Consumable items that were already used in-game are the most commonly refused category on both stores.

If a purchase is younger than 48 hours, act immediately — the 48-hour Google window is by far the easiest refund path in mobile gaming.

## Unauthorized Charges and Purchases by Children

Charges you (or a family member) did not authorize follow a different track than ordinary refunds, and the stores generally treat them more sympathetically when reported quickly:

- **Report it to the store first**, with the transaction ID, date, and amount. Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Sony all have dedicated unauthorized-charge flows.
- **Set up purchase authentication** going forward: require Face ID / fingerprint or a password for every purchase, and enable purchase approval settings in family accounts so kids cannot buy without a parent's OK.
- **Change your account password** and review linked payment methods if someone else had access to the device or account.

Approval is common for a single unauthorized charge reported promptly; a pattern of "buyer's remorse" claims is not.

## When to Contact FunPlus Support Instead

FunPlus publishes Aniimo and operates its own customer service, but as a rule the store that took your money is the one that refunds it. Contact official FunPlus support when:

- The store refused a refund and you believe a billing error occurred (double charge, wrong item delivered, currency not granted after a completed purchase).
- Your purchase is missing in-game — items bought but never delivered are a publisher-side fulfillment issue, not a refund case.
- You need an account issue documented before escalating with a store.

Always use the support channels linked from the official Aniimo website — the game's Discord and social accounts point to the real ones. Impostor "customer support" accounts in reply threads and DMs are a well-known scam pattern for popular free-to-play games, and no legitimate support agent will ever ask for your password or a code from your authenticator app. And as always with new free-to-play launches: ignore third-party "refund services" and currency resellers entirely, the same way you would ignore fake [Aniimo code](/release/aniimo-codes/) offers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I refund Aniimo?

The base game costs nothing — Aniimo is free-to-play on every platform, so there is no game purchase to refund. Refund questions only apply to optional purchases such as mobile in-app purchases or any packs and battle pass content offered after launch.

### How do I refund an Aniimo purchase on Steam?

Steam refunds purchases made within the last 14 days with under 2 hours of relevant playtime. Open help.steampowered.com, select the purchase, and choose a refund reason. Store credit arrives fast; card refunds take a few business days.

### How do I refund Aniimo purchases on iPhone or Android?

On iOS, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com and request a refund — Apple decides case by case. On Android, Google Play offers refunds within 48 hours of purchase; after that, request one through Play support. Consumed in-game currency is often refused on both stores.

### Can I refund a purchase on PS5 or Xbox?

On PlayStation, digital purchases can generally be canceled within 14 days if you have not started downloading them — add-on content is typically not refundable. On Xbox, Microsoft offers refunds for digital items within 14 days if they are unused or not significantly consumed.

### How do I get a refund for a child's accidental Aniimo purchase?

Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Sony all handle unauthorized-purchase requests through their support channels, and approvals are common with prompt reports. Contact the store directly with the transaction details rather than requesting a normal refund.

### Does FunPlus issue refunds directly?

FunPlus is the publisher and operates customer support, but refunds for store-bought items are normally processed by the store you bought from — Steam, Epic, PlayStation, Xbox, Apple, or Google. Go to the store first; contact official FunPlus support for billing issues the store will not resolve.
