If a new test opens before the September 16, 2026 launch, the steps below are how access has worked:

1. **Visit the Official Website:** Head to [aniimo.com](https://www.aniimo.com/) and create a FunPlus account.
2. **Complete Your Profile:** Fill out your hardware specifications and gaming preferences. Developers often send beta invites based on the hardware testers are using to ensure performance metrics are accurate.
3. **Join the Official Discord:** Beta invites, announcement notifications, and key giveaways frequently happen in the official [Aniimo Discord](https://discord.com/invite/aniimo).
