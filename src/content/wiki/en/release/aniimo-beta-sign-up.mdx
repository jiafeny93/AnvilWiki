## Step-by-Step: Aniimo Beta Sign Up Process

Getting into a closed beta test (CBT) requires a bit of patience, but the actual registration process is straightforward. Currently, the developers are running global tests to stress servers and gather gameplay feedback on the real-time combat and creature-bonding mechanics. 

Here is how you can complete your Aniimo beta sign up:

1. **Visit the Official Website:** Navigate to the official Aniimo website (https://www.aniimo.com/). This is the primary hub for all global pre-registration and beta testing links.
2. **Create or Link an Account:** You will need a valid publisher or platform account. Follow the prompts to log in using your preferred platform credentials (such as Steam, Xbox, or a dedicated FunPlus account, depending on the specific test phase).
3. **Complete the Questionnaire (If Applicable):** Some closed beta phases require players to fill out a short survey detailing their gaming preferences, hardware specs, and past experience with creature-collecting games. Be honest and thorough to increase your approval odds.
4. **Opt-In to Email Communications:** Ensure you agree to receive emails. Beta keys, download instructions, and server schedules are almost always dispatched directly to the email address associated with your application.
5. **Join the Official Discord:** For many modern games, the developers use Discord to distribute beta access or host opt-in channels. Join the official Aniimo Discord server (https://discord.com/invite/aniimo) and look for beta-specific channels.

## Current Aniimo Beta Phases and Platforms

Aniimo has been confirmed for a wide array of platforms, but that does not mean every beta test is available on every system simultaneously. Tests are usually rolled out in waves to manage server capacity and test specific hardware performance.

When you complete the Aniimo beta sign up, keep in mind which platform you are applying for. PC tests (via Steam or Epic Games Store) are generally the most frequent during early development phases due to easier patch deployment, while console tests for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S typically occur closer to launch. Mobile tests (iOS and Android) are also expected but have historically had stricter device requirements for early builds.

## Aniimo Beta Sign Up: Troubleshooting and Tips

Because the demand for the game is incredibly high—with pre-registrations already surpassing 5 million players—getting beta access is highly competitive. If you are struggling to get a key after completing the Aniimo beta sign up, here are a few tips:

- **Check Your Spam Folder:** Beta invitations often get caught in email spam filters. Search your inbox for emails from FunPlus or Pawprint Studio.
- **Verify Region Restrictions:** Some tests are region-locked for latency testing purposes. Make sure your region is supported before applying.
- **Monitor Official Channels:** Developers frequently drop surprise beta keys or extend testing windows via their official X (Twitter) account (@Aniimo_EN) and YouTube channel.
- **Ensure Hardware Compliance:** If you applied for a PC beta, ensure your machine meets the expected minimum specs for the current test, as the developers may filter applications based on hardware surveys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Aniimo beta free to play?

Yes, the Aniimo closed beta is completely free to access. However, you must receive an invitation through the official sign-up process; you cannot purchase access into the beta test.

### Can I sign up for the Aniimo beta on mobile?

You can express interest in mobile platforms (iOS and Android) through the official pre-registration, but specific closed beta tests may be restricted to PC or certain consoles depending on the phase. Always check the current beta requirements before applying.

### When is the next Aniimo beta test?

Exact dates for future beta phases are TBA and usually announced shortly before they go live. The most recent test was the [Second Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-beta-2) in January 2026, and the full launch is confirmed for September 16, 2026.

### Does pre-registering guarantee beta access?

No, global pre-registration guarantees exclusive launch rewards as the community hits milestones, but it does not guarantee you will be selected for the closed beta.

### What platforms are supported for the Aniimo beta?

Aniimo is officially coming to Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Mobile. However, specific beta tests may be limited to certain platforms like PC to streamline early development testing.

### Will my beta progress carry over to the full game?

It is highly unlikely. Most closed beta tests for live-service and online RPGs require server wipes before launch to ensure a fair start for all players at release. Expect your progress to be deleted after the test.
