Free-to-play launches of this size almost always open a pre-load window, because millions of simultaneous first-day downloads are exactly what store CDNs are built to smooth out. None of that is guaranteed — treat September 15 as a planning assumption, not a promise. The moment any store flips a pre-load switch, this page gets updated with the exact platform steps.

## Launch-Hour Checklist: Be Ready Before the Unlock

Preloading only saves time if the rest of your setup is ready too. Run through this list the night before the September 16 unlock:

- **Free the disk space.** Official sizes are TBA, but an open-world ARPG of this scope is realistically a 60-100 GB install on PC and consoles. Clear buffer now, not at 2:00 AM.
- **Update your platform software.** Console firmware updates and a Steam client update often land in the final 24 hours and queue behind everything else if you skip them early.
- **Sign in and link your accounts ahead of time.** Fumbling password resets during the launch-hour login queue is the classic self-inflicted delay.
- **Wishlist and pre-register everywhere you plan to play.** That is what triggers install notifications on Steam and store notifications on mobile.
- **Bookmark the server status page.** First-48-hour queues and login errors are the norm for online free-to-play launches; that page separates an official outage from your local network in minutes.
- **Ignore "play early" offers.** With beta servers closed since July 25, any mirror client, private server, or modified APK promising early access before the unlock is malware or a scam — the only legitimate entry is the official unlock itself.
