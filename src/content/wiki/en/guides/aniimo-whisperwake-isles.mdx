The wait is over — **Whisperwake Isles is live on October 29, 2026**. After maintenance ran from 07:00 to 15:00 UTC+8, Aniimo's first major content update opened the new archipelago as **Season 1 Part 2**, bringing four new regions, a wave of new creatures and [Prismana](/guides/aniimo-prismana/) forms, and an event calendar that runs through **December 10, 2026**. Everything on this page is confirmed from the official update notice and the official Aniimo Index, which refreshed on launch day.

Registered Pathfinders can claim **500 Glimmer** as maintenance compensation — check your in-game mailbox if it has not been delivered automatically.

## What Is Live Today

The official update notice confirms the launch-day facts:

- **The map is open.** Whisperwake Isles unlocks through the prerequisite story quests, and it spans four regions — **Crescent Bay, Rosy Mirelands, Wildwater Gorge and Cloudreach Falls**. The seasonal events additionally require finishing the story chapter **A Tale of Tide and Song**, which follows the little Aniimo Lyrelle and the youth Thesseo.
- **Two growth-stage caps rise.** Trailblazer II lifts the Aniimo level cap from 60 to 65, and Trailblazer III pushes both character and Aniimo caps to 70, with Elite Training gaining a "Chapter of Whisperwake" track.
- **The season window is six weeks.** Events run through December 10, so catch windows, rewards and probably fresh [codes](/release/aniimo-codes/) will keep arriving well past launch day.

One caveat: community trackers also report a **Grade I Trailblazer** rank as part of the map unlock. The official notice names the story requirement; if the map stays gated after you finish the story, grind your Trailblazer rank next.

## New Aniimo: The Confirmed List

The update adds about 51 creatures in total — 32 species and 19 variants. The official notice names these first arrivals:

### Season Prismana Aniimo (4)

The headliners are four season-exclusive Prismana Aniimo, each on a weekly rotation in the "Vein Abundance" event — the same Prismana system covered in our [Prismana guide](/guides/aniimo-prismana/):

| Aniimo | Vein Abundance window | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Prismana Pouchwisp | November 2 – 9 | Rosy Mirelands / Tideblossom Coast |
| Prismana Royalby | November 9 – 16 | Wildwater Gorge / Sea of Flowers |
| Prismana Jeburst | November 16 – 23 | Cloudreach Falls / Russet Highlands |
| Prismana Glameep | November 23 – 30 | Crescent Bay / Echoback Landing |
| All four together | November 30 – December 7 | Their home regions |

### New species on the official Index

The official Aniimo Index already carries full data for four brand-new creatures, and we have dedicated pages live for each: [Squarrel](/creatures/aniimo-squarrel/) and its evolution [Squashel](/creatures/aniimo-squashel/), the Fire Support [Sparkelf](/creatures/aniimo-sparkelf/) with a Prismana form of its own, and the legendary Light DPS [Lunara](/creatures/aniimo-lunara/).

Two existing creatures moved into a new Index section with launch: Dazmand now sits in the 10002 slot and Fulmintis in 10003 — and Fulmintis's Prismana form, with its Light + Lightning typing, now has its own full index page. Existing favorites Gachapus, Sugartini, Jabster and Whirlbite also gained new regional Prismana forms in the isles, per the official notice.

### Companion and event Aniimo

- **Hypnyto** — joins via the Companion Handbook; Wind element, DPS role, with a Prismana Hypnyto form.
- **Ekrim** — the season achievement reward "Prismana Ekrim"; Dark element, Breaker role.
- **Jaguaraun** — the co-created Aniimo; Lightning element, Breaker role, arriving through the Fantasy Resonance event on November 20.

Community trackers additionally list **Saws and Prawnch** among the launch arrivals — the official notice does not name them yet, so treat those two as reported rather than confirmed.

## What to Do First

The launch-day checklist, in priority order:

1. **Claim your 500 Glimmer compensation**, then clear the prerequisite story quests until the isles gate opens.
2. **Work the first Vein Abundance window** — Prismana Pouchwisp is catchable from November 2. Miss a week and that Prismana waits until the November 30 all-four window.
3. **Bank resources as you explore.** The new [creatures](/creatures/) mean new catches to level — stockpile Credits and Growth Flowers, and redeem the [active codes](/release/aniimo-codes/) before any expire.
4. **Recheck your team.** New regions usually mean new elements to fight. Run your lineup through the [team coverage checker](/guides/aniimo-team-coverage/) with the [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/) as the reference.
5. **Save Glimmer for the new banners.** Season launches typically bring rate-up banners for the new arrivals — spend after the official banner list lands, not before.

We will keep this page updated as the official Index fills in the remaining unnamed arrivals over the coming days.
