Aniimo's first major content update is days away. **Whisperwake Isles opens on October 29, 2026** as Season 1 Part 2, adding a new region, a wave of new creatures, and new [Prismana](/guides/aniimo-prismana/) forms — with season events scheduled to run through **December 9, 2026**. Here is everything confirmed so far, clearly separated from what community trackers report, and a preparation checklist so the new map opens into a ready account instead of a scramble.

A note on sources before the details: developer patch notes for the update are not out yet. The facts below are cross-checked between gaming-news coverage and community tracker sites, and anything that comes only from trackers is labeled as reported. Nothing on this page is guesswork.

> **Status: October 23, 2026** — release date and region name confirmed by multiple outlets; creature names and unlock requirements reported by community trackers, subject to change on launch day.

## What Is Confirmed About Whisperwake Isles

Two facts are safe to plan around:

- **Release date: October 29, 2026.** Multiple news outlets date the update — branded **Season 1 Part 2** — to October 29, the same week as the game's [October 15 patch](/guides/aniimo-walkthrough/) wrapped up its Home Season event.
- **Scale: about 51 new creatures.** Coverage of the update cites 32 new species and 19 variants. Even if the final count shifts, this is the largest single content drop since the [September launch](/release/aniimo-release-date/), and the biggest long-tail wave of new [creatures](/creatures/) the game has had.

The season window matters as much as the date: events are scheduled through **December 9**, which means catch windows, event rewards and probably limited [codes](/release/aniimo-codes/) will keep arriving for six weeks after launch day.

## How to Prepare Before October 29

The checklist, in priority order:

1. **Finish the story requirements now.** Trackers report the isles unlock at **Grade I Trailblazer** after the story chapter **A Tale of Tide and Song**. Both take time — doing them this week means you walk into the new map on day one instead of grinding behind a gate.
2. **Bank your resources.** New regions mean new catches to level: stack **Credits** and **Growth Flowers**. Redeem the [active codes](/release/aniimo-codes/) first — `ANIIMOGIFT` alone is worth 20,000 Credits, and event windows historically come with fresh codes you do not want to miss.
3. **Square away your team.** New creatures usually mean new elements to fight. Run your lineup through the [team coverage checker](/guides/aniimo-team-coverage/) to see which attacking types you cannot produce and which defensive weaknesses you are carrying, with the [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/) as the reference.
4. **Save pull currency for the new banners.** Season launches typically bring rate-up banners for the new arrivals. If you have been sitting on Glimmer, the week of October 29 is the conventional time to spend it — though wait for the official banner list before committing.
