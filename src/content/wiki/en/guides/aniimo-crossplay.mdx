One of Aniimo's headline promises since its 2025 reveal: play the same world with anyone, on anything. The game launches [globally on September 16, 2026](/release/aniimo-release-date/) as a [free-to-play](/release/aniimo-free-to-play/) title launching September 16, 2026 on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with mobile (iOS and Android) following on September 23 — and [cross-platform play](/release/aniimo-platforms/) was designed in from the start rather than bolted on. Here is exactly what is confirmed, what is still likely-but-TBA, and what it means for your friend group spanning PC and phones.

## What Is Confirmed

| Feature | Status | Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cross-platform play | **Confirmed** | PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile share the same ecosystem |
| Co-op multiplayer | **Confirmed** | Online co-op quests are part of the base game — see the [multiplayer guide](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) |
| One client per platform family | **Confirmed** | Steam and Epic on PC; Xbox Series X|S with Xbox Play Anywhere support |
| Cross-progression | **TBA — likely** | Not officially confirmed; the shared-world design strongly implies it |
| Input-based matchmaking | **TBA** | How mobile and PC players mix in harder content is unannounced |

The foundation goes back to the game's first showing: developer [Pawprint Studio](/release/aniimo-developer/) pitched PC, console, and mobile as equal launch targets from day one, and the closed beta program in 2026 tested across all of them. This is not a mobile game promising a desktop port later, or the reverse — it is one build family designed as a cross-platform world.

## Cross-Progression — the Part Everyone Actually Cares About

Crossplay answers "can I play with my friends?" Cross-progression answers the follow-up: "can I start on my PC and continue on my phone?" For Aniimo the honest answer today is **expected, but not yet officially confirmed**.

The reasoning behind the expectation: Aniimo is structured as one shared live world — a persistent open world ([Idyll](/guides/aniimo-exploration/)) where [co-op quests](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/), trading-style systems like the [exchange](/guides/aniimo-exchange/), and live-service events all assume players on any device share the same economy and social space. A shared world with per-platform silos would fragment every one of those systems. The [platform plan](/release/aniimo-platforms/) also leans that way, with cross-platform progression listed as highly likely for the mobile-and-PC pairing.

What is missing is the explicit statement. Account systems for live-service games occasionally restrict progress sharing between platform ecosystems (console makers have their own policies), so until Pawprint or FunPlus confirms it in a launch-feature list, this page keeps cross-progression at "likely" — and updates the moment it is confirmed either way.

## How Parties Work Across Platforms

The co-op structure is the [multiplayer guide's](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) territory — this is the cross-platform slice:

- **Party up by friends list or invite.** Whether the friends list itself is unified under one Aniimo account or per-platform is part of the same account-system question as cross-progression — TBA, same answer, same update schedule.
- **Co-op quests scale for mixed groups.** Nothing in the announced design gates co-op by platform — a phone player and a PC player joining the same quest is the intended flow, and the beta tests ran exactly these mixed groups.
- **Voice chat and social features** — platform-standard expectations apply; details on cross-platform voice are TBA.

## Platform-by-Platform Notes

- **PC (Steam / Epic Games Store):** both storefronts at launch, and [Steam](/release/aniimo-steam/) wishlisting is live now. PC players get the full settings suite — see the [system requirements](/release/aniimo-system-requirements/) before launch day.
- **PlayStation 5:** a day-one launch platform — the [PS5 edition details](/release/aniimo-ps5/) cover pre-load and account linkage as they are announced.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** supports Xbox Play Anywhere, which matters for the Xbox-PC pairing — one license across both Microsoft ecosystems.
- **Mobile (iOS / Android):** the same world in your pocket; the [mobile version](/release/aniimo-mobile/) is a full client, not a companion app, and the [APK install path](/release/aniimo-apk-download/) exists for Android users outside store regions.

## What to Do Before Launch (Crossplay Prep)

1. **Pre-register on every platform you might use** — the [pre-registration](/release/aniimo-pre-register/) rewards attach to launch and cost nothing.
2. **Sort storage now** — especially on mobile and base consoles; the install footprint grows with the day-one patch. Requirements are broken down per platform in the [system requirements guide](/release/aniimo-system-requirements/).
3. **Settle your account question early** — if a unified Aniimo account system is announced before launch, link it before day one. Account linking during launch congestion is exactly when things go wrong; the [error-code guide](/release/aniimo-error-codes/) documents why.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Aniimo have crossplay?

Yes. Aniimo supports cross-platform play across PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile (iOS and Android). PC and consoles launch on September 16, 2026, with mobile following on September 23 — playing with friends on a different platform than yours is a designed-in feature, not an afterthought.

### Does Aniimo have cross-progression?

Cross-progression is expected but not yet officially confirmed. Aniimo is built as one shared world across platforms, and the platform plan strongly implies shared progress — but until the developer or publisher states it outright, treat cross-progression as likely rather than guaranteed.

### Is Aniimo crossplay free?

Yes. Aniimo is a free-to-play game on every platform, and online co-op multiplayer is part of the base experience — there is no paid tier required to play with friends on other platforms. Optional purchases are cosmetic and convenience-focused.

### Can mobile players play with PC players in Aniimo?

Yes — mobile, PC, and console players share the same cross-platform ecosystem at launch. Details like input-based matchmaking or control fairness options are TBA, so how mobile and PC players are grouped in competitive content may still change.
