The Aniimo exchange is the game's player-to-player creature trading system — the feature that lets one Pathfinder hand an Aniimo to another. It was officially introduced in Aniimo's [Global Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-global-closed-beta/) (July 9–25, 2026), where the announcement confirmed that "Pathfinders now have the ability to exchange Aniimo with each other." For a creature-collector, that single sentence is huge: player-to-player trading shapes the entire endgame economy, rare-hunting, and co-op play. Here's what's officially known, what's still TBA, and what to watch out for.

## Why Trading Changes Aniimo's Endgame

Creature games live or die on what players can do with duplicates and rare pulls. An exchange system ripples through every corner of the game:

- **Duplicate Aniimo become currency.** Catching your fifth [Bubbeep](/creatures/aniimo-bubbeep/) stops being dead weight — it becomes trade stock for something you haven't caught.
- **Co-op teams can specialize.** Two Pathfinders can split coverage — one tanks, one deals damage — and trade each other creatures that fit their builds, on top of what the [Inherit system](/guides/aniimo-inherit-system/) already does for team synergy.
- **Rare variants get a market.** If rare [Prismana](/guides/aniimo-prismana/) variants are exchangeable, shiny-hunting turns from a solo grind into a player economy. Whether they are tradeable is exactly the kind of detail that remains TBA.
- **Completionism gets faster.** A 94-creature Index plus event legendaries like [Irisalis](/creatures/aniimo-irisalis/) is a big dex to fill; trading is the classic shortcut — with official blessing instead of gray-market workarounds.

Every bullet above is analysis, not confirmed mechanics — the point is why the feature matters, not a promise of how it will work.

## Scam Warnings (Read Before Launch)

Any trade system spawns predators. Three rules that will hold regardless of the final mechanics:

1. **Trades happen in-game or not at all.** The exchange is a game feature; there is no legitimate off-platform "middleman" for Aniimo trades. Discord DMs offering to broker trades are scams in waiting.
2. **Beta trades are already gone.** The beta was a data-wipe test — every exchange made there evaporated on July 25, 2026. Anyone claiming to still hold beta creatures to trade is lying.
3. **Real money trades are unregulated and bannable.** Buying Aniimo from other players for cash has no official support and every risk (chargebacks, account theft, bans). Don't.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Aniimo exchange?
The Aniimo exchange is Aniimo's player-to-player creature trading feature, introduced in the Global Closed Beta (July 9-25, 2026). The official announcement confirmed that Pathfinders "now have the ability to exchange Aniimo with each other."

### Can you trade Aniimo with other players?
Yes — exchanging Aniimo with other Pathfinders was officially confirmed as Global Closed Beta content. The fine print (restrictions, costs, rarity limits) was never published, so how trading will work in the launch build remains TBA.

### How does the Aniimo exchange work?
TBA beyond the basics. Officially, players can exchange Aniimo with each other; nothing has been published about trade windows, level requirements, currency costs, or whether rare variants like Prismana Aniimo are tradeable. Details are expected around the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Is the Aniimo exchange the same as trading in Pokemon?
Mechanically it fills the same role — moving creatures between players — but no specifics have been confirmed, so the comparison stops there. Aniimo's exchange is real-time and online-first, and any deeper similarity (or difference) is speculation until launch.

### Was the exchange in the closed beta only?
It debuted in the Global Closed Beta. Trading revealed in a pre-launch test is generally expected to ship at launch, and it would be unusual to remove it — but no official statement confirms the exchange for the September 16, 2026 build, so treat launch availability as expected rather than confirmed.
