As a Pathfinder in the open world of Idyll, you will explore, battle, and bond with creatures alongside other players across PC, console, and mobile. ## Is Aniimo a Multiplayer Game? The Friends Basics

Yes — co-op is not a bolt-on mode, it is a design pillar. Pawprint Studio has consistently presented Aniimo as a shared-world open-world action RPG where you team up with other Pathfinders for fantasy quests, world bosses, and exploration. The full multiplayer picture lives in our [multiplayer guide](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/), but the short version for friends:

- **Shared open world:** You can roam the continent of Idyll with other players, not just instanced lobbies.
- **Co-op combat:** Real-time action combat built for group play — coordinating creature abilities across a party is the intended experience.
- **Crossplay designed-in:** PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile are all part of one ecosystem — see the [crossplay guide](/guides/aniimo-crossplay/).
- **Player trading exists:** The exchange system tested in the July 2026 [Global Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-global-closed-beta/) confirmed some form of player-to-player creature trading.

## How to Add Friends in Aniimo (Expected Launch Flow)

The exact friend-add screen has not been shown in official showcases, so treat this section as the likely flow rather than confirmed UI. Aniimo launches simultaneously on multiple storefronts, which means the friends system almost certainly has to work across platforms — and modern cross-play games handle that with an in-game account layer rather than platform-only friends lists. The realistic launch-day options:

| Method | How it works | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **In-game social menu search** | Search a player ID or username, send a friend request | Expected — standard for cross-play titles |
| **Platform friends list** | Pulls friends from Steam, PSN, Xbox, or your Google Play / App Store account | Expected as a supplement, not the core system |
| **Meet in the open world** | Walk up to a player, open their profile card, send an invite | Expected — shared-world games usually allow this |
| **Recent players list** | Add someone you just ran a quest or battle with | Possible, not confirmed |

Practical steps for launch day, then, look like this: link or sign into your platform account first (Steam, Epic, PSN, Xbox, or your mobile store account), reach the in-game social or community menu, and add friends by name from there. If Aniimo uses a unique player ID — many cross-platform games do — you will find it on your profile page. ## Crossplay Friends: Adding Players on Other Platforms

Aniimo's most ambitious promise is that your friend group does not need to own the same hardware. A Steam player, a PS5 player, an Xbox player, and an iPhone player are all meant to end up in the same world. Cross-platform play was designed in from the start rather than bolted on — our [crossplay guide](/guides/aniimo-crossplay/) has the full platform matrix.

What that means for friends specifically:

- **One shared ecosystem.** Cross-play is confirmed as a launch feature across PC, consoles, and mobile, so adding a friend on a different platform should work through the in-game system rather than requiring you to both own the same storefront.
- **Cross-progression is likely but TBA.** The multi-platform design heavily implies shared progression — start on PC, continue on mobile — but the developers have not fully detailed cross-save yet. Do not assume your progress follows you until it is confirmed.
- **Mobile joins one week later.** PC and consoles unlock on September 16, 2026; mobile (iOS and Android) arrives on September 23, 2026. Your crossplay friend group only completes when the mobile wave lands.

## Playing Together: What You Can Actually Do With Friends

Once your friends list is populated, Aniimo's co-op loop kicks in. Based on official showcases and the closed beta tests, here is what group play involves:

- **Co-op fantasy quests.** Team up for quest lines designed for groups, with real-time combat that rewards coordinating different creatures' abilities.
- **World bosses and group battles.** The Twine bonding system — seeing through your creature's eyes and wielding its abilities — is expected to shine in parties, where players chain abilities together against tough enemies.
- **Shared exploration.** Roam Idyll together, show off rare creature discoveries, and help friends in battles they are struggling with.
- **Trading via the exchange system.** The beta confirmed some form of player-to-player creature trading. Whether trading is restricted to friends, gated by progression, or open to all is still TBA.

## Friend List and Party Limits: What Is Still TBA

A few numbers matter to any friends system, and none are official yet:

- **Friend list cap** — not announced. Modern social games typically allow 100+ friends; Aniimo's limit should appear in launch materials.
- **Max party size** — not officially confirmed. A small co-op group of up to four players is the community expectation for open-world content, but treat it as an estimate.
- **Voice chat** — not detailed. Expect platform-level voice (parties on PSN/Xbox, Discord on PC) to carry the load at minimum.
- **Cross-progression** — expected, not confirmed. This is the single most-requested detail for mixed-platform friend groups.
