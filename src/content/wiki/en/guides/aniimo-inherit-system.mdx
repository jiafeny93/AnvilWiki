The Aniimo Inherit system is one of the most talked-about progression mechanics revealed for Aniimo, the open-world creature-catching action RPG from Pawprint Studio and FunPlus. First showcased during closed beta tests, Inherit lets your Aniimo companions inherit a share of your Pathfinder character's stats — meaning the gear and attributes you build for yourself can directly strengthen the creatures bonded to you through the Twine system.

Because Aniimo has not launched yet (global release is confirmed for September 16, 2026), every detail below reflects beta reveals and is subject to change. This guide breaks down what the Inherit system is, which stats are involved, how to build around it, and how it compares to other systems like [Prismana](/guides/aniimo-prismana/).

## What Is the Aniimo Inherit System?

The Aniimo Inherit system is an attribute-sharing mechanic in which your Aniimo companions inherit a portion of your player character's stats. It was visible in Aniimo's closed beta tests — most notably the [Second Closed Beta](/release/aniimo-beta-2/) in January 2026 — and is one of several systems that tie your Pathfinder's personal build to your team's overall power.

**Confirmed so far (from official beta tests):**

- Inherit exists as an attribute system where companions inherit a share of the Pathfinder's stats, with crit, HP, and defense explicitly shown in beta builds.
- Beta builds showed concrete values for at least one companion family — wolf-type Aniimo inherited 60% of the protagonist Sylvan's attributes (including crit rate and crit damage), plus 100% of HP and 100% of Defense.
- The system was active alongside the core [Twine](/guides/aniimo-twining/) bonding mechanic, which lets you bond with Aniimo, see through their eyes, and wield their abilities in real-time combat.
- The official wiki at wiki.aniimo.com is the place to verify beta-era data on the system.

**Rumored or expected (not confirmed):**

- Whether the flow is strictly one-directional (companion inherits from player) or can work in reverse.
- Whether individual Aniimo species or Twine bond levels modify how much is inherited.

Beta builds did show hard numbers — for example, wolves inheriting 60% of Sylvan's attributes plus 100% HP and 100% Defense — so treat those as beta-era values that may be retuned before launch.

## How the Aniimo Inherit System Works

Based on what has been shown, Inherit functions as a bridge between your character's equipment/stats and your companions' effectiveness. Because Aniimo's [combat](/guides/aniimo-combat/) is real-time and you fight alongside your bonded creatures, boosting your Pathfinder's attributes is expected to do double duty: it improves your own performance while simultaneously raising the floor of your Aniimo's stats.

The stats demonstrated in beta include:

| Pathfinder Stat | Expected Effect on Your Aniimo | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Crit (rate/damage) | Amplifies companion critical hits and damage output | Shown in beta reveals |
| HP | Improves companion survivability in fights | Shown in beta reveals |
| Defense | Reduces damage your companions take | Shown in beta reveals |
| Attack / other attributes | Likely inherited as well, based on system design | Unconfirmed — TBA |

The word "share" is key: companions appear to receive a fraction of your stats rather than a full copy, so your Aniimo will still need their own growth (levels, skills, and bond progression) to reach their ceiling. Inherit looks like a multiplier on top of companion progression, not a replacement for it.

## How to Build Your Pathfinder Around Inheritance

If the Aniimo Inherit system ships close to its beta form, your build priorities as a Pathfinder change meaningfully. Investing in your own stats is no longer purely selfish — it's party-wide investment. Here's how to think about it:

| Build Focus | Why It Matters with Inherit | What to Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Crit-focused gear | Crit stats shown in beta appear to carry over, multiplying damage across your whole team | Whether crit rate, crit damage, or both are inherited |
| HP / defense stacking | Tankier Pathfinders likely field tankier companions, useful in harder open-world fights | Whether defensive inheritance has diminishing returns |
| Balanced spread | Safest default before exact conversion rates are known | Beta tuning may reward specialization instead |

A few practical takeaways while details are still fluid:

1. **Don't neglect your own equipment.** In beta framing, your Pathfinder is effectively another gear slot for your companions.
2. **Match your build to your favorite Aniimo.** If your go-to companion is a durable frontline fighter, HP and defense inheritance may matter more than crit.
3. **Re-evaluate after launch.** Percentages and inheritable stat lists are exactly the kind of values that get retuned between beta and release, so verify against the official wiki and patch notes once Aniimo goes live.

For which companions deserve that investment, see the [Aniimo tier list](/tier-list/aniimo-tier-list/).

## Inherit vs. Other Aniimo Progression Systems

Inherit doesn't exist in a vacuum. Here's how it fits alongside the other major systems revealed so far:

| System | What It Does | Confirmed Status |
|---|---|---|
| Inherit | Companions inherit a share of your Pathfinder's stats (crit, HP, defense shown) | Beta-only; details may change at launch |
| Twine | Core bonding system — link with Aniimo, see through their eyes, use their abilities in real-time combat | Confirmed core mechanic |
| Prismana | Rare rainbow variants tied to [Prismana Flow weather](/guides/aniimo-prismana/) | Beta-only; subject to change |
| Companion leveling & skills | Standard per-creature growth expected in a creature-catching RPG | Expected, specifics TBA |

The likely synergy picture: Twine determines which abilities you can wield and how deeply you bond, companion leveling raises each creature's base power, and Inherit layers your character's stats on top. Prismana, by contrast, is a rarity/collecting layer and shouldn't be confused with stat inheritance.

## FAQ

**How does the Inherit system work in Aniimo?**

Inherit works by letting your Aniimo companions inherit a share of your Pathfinder character's stats, such as crit, HP, and defense. It effectively makes your own gear and stat build strengthen your bonded creatures too.

**Is the Aniimo Inherit system confirmed for launch?**

The system itself was shown in official beta tests, but its exact values and rules may change before the September 16, 2026 global launch. The Second Closed Beta showed concrete numbers — wolf companions inherited 60% of the protagonist Sylvan's attributes plus full HP and Defense — so treat them as beta-era data until launch values are confirmed.

**Which stats can Aniimo companions inherit?**

Based on beta reveals, companions can inherit crit, HP, and defense from your Pathfinder. Other stats may also be inheritable, but the full list is unconfirmed and TBA.

**Do stats inherit both ways between my character and my Aniimo?**

As shown in beta, inheritance appears one-directional — companions inherit a share of the player character's stats. Whether your Pathfinder can gain anything back from companions has not been confirmed.

**How do I make my companions stronger through the Inherit system?**

Invest in your own Pathfinder's gear and stats, since crit, HP, and defense appear to carry over to your Aniimo. Pair that with normal companion growth like leveling and Twine bond progression for the best results.

**Does Inherit work in multiplayer co-op?**

Co-op is a confirmed design pillar, but whether inherited stats apply identically in multiplayer groups has not been detailed. Assume the system applies to your character globally until official channels say otherwise.
