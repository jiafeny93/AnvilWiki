## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Tuckin's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Tuckin with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Tuckin on a line with [Hummin](/creatures/aniimo-hummin/) (stage 1) → [Witchin](/creatures/aniimo-witchin/) (stage 3) → **Tuckin** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Tuckin in Aniimo?

Tuckin is a Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.
