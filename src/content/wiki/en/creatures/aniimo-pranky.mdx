## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Pranky's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Healer tag means its kit keeps the squad alive through sustain and recovery skills, freeing your other Aniimo slots for pure offense.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Pranky on a line with [Skippy](/creatures/aniimo-skippy/) (stage 1) → **Pranky** (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Pranky in Aniimo?

Pranky is a Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the Healer role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Pranky in Aniimo?

Pranky appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Nimbus Fields, Mistwoods, Forest of Falling Stars), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Skippy. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Pranky evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Pranky to Skippy. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Pranky a good Aniimo to use?

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Pranky fits the Healer role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
