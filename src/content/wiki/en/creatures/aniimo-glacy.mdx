## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Glacy's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Glacy carries a dual Water/Ice typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Healer tag means its kit keeps the squad alive through sustain and recovery skills, freeing your other Aniimo slots for pure offense.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Glacy in Aniimo?

Glacy is a Water/Ice Aniimo in the official index, filling the Healer role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

Glacy's habitats aren't indexed yet. Like every Aniimo, you'll weaken it in real-time combat and bond with it through the Twine system once locations are confirmed.
