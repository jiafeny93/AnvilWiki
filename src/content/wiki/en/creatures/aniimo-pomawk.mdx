## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Pomawk's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Pomawk with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

The official evolution tree places Pomawk on a line with [Pomegg](/creatures/aniimo-pomegg/) (stage 1) → [Dazmand](/creatures/aniimo-dazmand/) (stage 3) → **Pomawk** (stage 3). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Pomawk in Aniimo?

Pomawk is a Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Pomawk in Aniimo?

Pomawk appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Rosetower Woods), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Pomegg. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Pomawk evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Pomawk to Pomegg, Dazmand. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Pomawk a good Aniimo to use?

With BREAK as its strongest base attribute, Pomawk fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
