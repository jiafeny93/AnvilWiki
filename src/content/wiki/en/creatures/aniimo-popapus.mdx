## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Popapus's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Popapus on a line with **Popapus** (stage 1) → [Gachapus](/creatures/aniimo-gachapus/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Gachapon:** Each basic attack and skill triggers the gacha once, randomly firing an orange, purple, blue, or gold bubble and adding that bubble color to the gacha slots. Gold bubbles have a 1% chance to appear, while each other color has a 33% chance to appear. When the slots hold 5 bubbles of the same color, or when a gold bubble appears, all bubbles are cleared and [Jackpot] begins for 10s. In [Jackpot], basic attacks and skills no longer trigger the gacha and always fire Rainbow Bubbles. Basic attack damage +60%.

## Where to Find Popapus

The official index lists Popapus in **Crescent Bay**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Shelly](/creatures/aniimo-shelly/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

Building around the Water element? [Piopiota](/creatures/aniimo-piopiota/) is another confirmed Water pick.
