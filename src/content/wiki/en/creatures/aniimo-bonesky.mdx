## Element and Role

As an Ice creature, Bonesky's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Lightning and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Ice, Earth and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Bonesky on a line with **Bonesky** (stage 1) → [Fenrier](/creatures/aniimo-fenrier/) (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

The official index lists Bonesky in **Beast Fang Ridge**, **Driftwise Meadow**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Scorchhowl](/creatures/aniimo-scorchhowl/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Bonesky is a Ice Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.
