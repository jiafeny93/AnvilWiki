## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Erlath's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Erlath on a line with [Fahloo](/creatures/aniimo-fahloo/) (stage 1) → **Erlath** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Erlath in Aniimo?

Erlath is a Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the Regen role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Erlath in Aniimo?

Erlath appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Tideblossom Coast), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Fahloo. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Erlath evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Erlath to Fahloo. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Erlath fits the Regen role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
