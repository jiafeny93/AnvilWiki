## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Glameep's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Glameep carries a dual Grass/Water typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Healer tag means its kit keeps the squad alive through sustain and recovery skills, freeing your other Aniimo slots for pure offense.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Glameep on a line with [Bubbeep](/creatures/aniimo-bubbeep/) (stage 1) → **Glameep** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
