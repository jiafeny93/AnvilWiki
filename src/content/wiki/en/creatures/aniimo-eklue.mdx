## Aniimo Eklue Combat Abilities and the Twine System

The real-time combat of Aniimo sets it apart from traditional creature-catching games, and the Aniimo Eklue is perfectly designed for this dynamic environment. ### Combat Role in Your Party

Based on the game's reveal at the 2025 Xbox Games Showcase and subsequent developer diaries, Eklue appears to fulfill the role of a DPS (Damage Per Second) or utility skirmisher. Its lithe frame and magical aura suggest it relies on quick dodges, rapid strikes, and short-cooldown magical attacks to overwhelm enemies. Unlike heavier, tank-like Aniimo (such as the heavily armored Helmut), Eklue is expected to reward aggressive, hit-and-run gameplay.

When controlling Eklue via the Twine system, players can expect to utilize a combination of light magical bursts and evasive maneuvers. This makes it an incredibly valuable asset in Aniimo's co-op [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) fantasy quests, where you and your friends can coordinate your bonded creatures to take down massive world bosses.
