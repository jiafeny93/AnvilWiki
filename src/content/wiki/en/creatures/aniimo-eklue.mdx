## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Eklue's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Eklue rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Eklue at the top of the line with [Eko](/creatures/aniimo-eko/) (stage 1) → **Eklue** (stage 3). The jump is the biggest stat leap in the line (440 → 518) and unlocks the innate Target Weakness below. Base-form investment carries forward through the family, so an Eko raised early arrives here with its progress intact.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Judgment Storm:** When a skill applies a debuff to a target, Wind damage for the whole team increases by 20% for 15s.

**Combat skills:**

| Skill | Cost | Power |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Detect Weakness | 0 | 0 |
| Tactical Kick | 20 | 45 |
| Ice Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |
| Water Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |
| Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |

**Innate skills:** **ATK**, **Target Weakness**.

The kit in one breath: **Detect Weakness** holds a target's weak point centered for 1 second to apply Weakness Mark (−7% Crit Resistance for 20s); the arc strikes stun anything marked for 1.5 seconds; **Tactical Kick** dashes in, backflip-kicks, and hops back out with a 30% slow attached; and the innate **Target Weakness** marks *every target in view range* for 30s while shaving 12% off their Wind Resistance. Every mark procs Judgment Storm. Skill costs are the resource spend shown on the official sheet; power is its listed base value. For how skills interact with the Twine system, see the [combat guide](/guides/aniimo-combat/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Eklue in Aniimo?

Eklue is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Eklue in Aniimo?

Eklue appears in the wild in the Forest of Falling Stars, where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As the stage-3 form of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Eko. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Eklue evolve from Eko?

Yes — the official evolution tree runs Eko (stage 1) to Eklue (stage 3). Eklue is the final form of the line and shares Eko's Judgment Storm trait.

### What does Eklue's innate Target Weakness do?

Target Weakness applies Weakness Mark to every target within view range for 30 seconds and reduces their Wind Resistance by 12 percent for 30 seconds — a whole-screen setup that its arc strikes punish with stuns and that Judgment Storm converts into a team-wide Wind damage buff.

### Is Eklue good against stealth or cloaked enemies?

Its official flavor text says the tail ring amplifies ultrasound to see through cloaked creatures, and its sonar kit is built around detecting and marking weak points — the closest thing in the published roster to an anti-stealth specialist.
