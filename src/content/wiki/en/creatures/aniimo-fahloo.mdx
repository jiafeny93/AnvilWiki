## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Fahloo's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Fahloo on a line with **Fahloo** (stage 1) → [Erlath](/creatures/aniimo-erlath/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.
