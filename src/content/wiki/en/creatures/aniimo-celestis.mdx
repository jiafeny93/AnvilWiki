## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Celestis's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Celestis on a line with **Celestis** (stage 1) → [Stellarys](/creatures/aniimo-stellarys/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Where to Find Celestis

The official index lists Celestis in **Nimbus Fields**, **The Argent Strait**, **Echoback Landing**, **Forest of Falling Stars**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Turbo](/creatures/aniimo-turbo/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.
