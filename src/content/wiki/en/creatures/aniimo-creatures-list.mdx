## Understanding the Twine Bonding System

In most creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) games, you throw an object to capture a monster. Aniimo flips this script entirely with the **Twine** (联结) bonding system. Instead of forcing a creature into a storage device, Pathfinders forge a mental and spiritual bond with the Aniimo. 

Once Twine is established, you can seamlessly switch between controlling your human Pathfinder and syncing directly with your Aniimo. When synced, the camera shifts, and you can literally see through the eyes of the creature, controlling their movements and unleashing their unique abilities in real-time action combat. This mechanic is set to revolutionize exploration, as different Aniimo will have different traversal skills—such as flying, swimming, or smashing obstacles—that you must use to solve environmental puzzles.

While the full scope is unconfirmed, bonding is expected to rely on trust-building mechanics, exploration, and helping creatures in the wild rather than spamming capture items.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Twine bonding system in Aniimo?

The Twine system is Aniimo's unique mechanic that replaces traditional creature capturing. Instead of trapping creatures, you forge a bond that allows you to sync with them, see through their eyes, and control their unique abilities directly in real-time combat.

### How many creatures are in the Aniimo creatures list?

The official Aniimo Index lists 94 creatures as of August 18, 2026 (No.001–No.093 plus two unnumbered teaser entries, with No.013 still hidden). More reveals and post-launch additions are expected over the game's lifetime.

### Will Aniimo have evolutions for its creatures?

While not officially detailed, it is highly expected that Aniimo will feature evolution or progression lines. These upgrades will likely be tied to your Twine bond level, unlocking new abilities and visual transformations as you progress.

### Can you play as the creatures in Aniimo?

Yes, you can. Using the Twine system, players can sync with their bonded Aniimo to directly control them. This allows you to utilize their specific combat skills and traversal mechanics, such as flying or swimming, to navigate the world.
