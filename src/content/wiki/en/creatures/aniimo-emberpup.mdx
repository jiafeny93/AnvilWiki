## Combat Abilities and the Twine System

What sets Aniimo apart from traditional RPGs is the **Twine** system. When you bond with an Aniimo Emberpup, you aren't just issuing commands from a menu. Pathfinders can tether to their creatures, seeing through their eyes and directly wielding their fire abilities in real-time combat.

While exact skill names and damage multipliers remain under development, Emberpup's combat loop is expected to revolve around aggressive, close-to-mid-range damage. Players who Twine with Emberpup will likely gain access to:

1.  **A standard fiery dash or lunging bite to close the distance on enemies.**
2.  **A localized flame burst to deal AoE damage and apply a burn status effect.**
3.  **Utility skills, such as lighting campfires or burning away thorny environmental obstacles blocking exploration paths in Idyll.**

Because combat happens in real-time, dodging, positioning, and knowing when to unleash Emberpup's abilities will be critical to mastering the game.
