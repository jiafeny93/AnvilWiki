## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Emberpup's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Emberpup at the head of the line with **Emberpup** (stage 1) → [Flameruff](/creatures/aniimo-flameruff/) (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family — worth knowing before you sink materials into your starter.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

Fire Kick and Pebble Kick are dodge-counter openers — jump, dodge an attack, then dive-kick everything nearby, with a successful dodge paying out bonus damage and a 20-second buff. Fire Bolt is the aimed poke that stacks Fire Debuff. Skill costs are the resource spend shown on the official sheet; power is its listed base value. ## Where to Find Emberpup

The official index lists Emberpup in **Nimbus Fields**, **Mistwoods**, **The Argent Strait**, **Echoback Landing** and **Beast Fang Ridge** — five zones, the widest habitat net in the published roster. Whatever biome you start in, an Emberpup is plausibly nearby. Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

Building around the Fire element? [Scorchhowl](/creatures/aniimo-scorchhowl/) is the No.003 Fire DPS up the damage curve.

## Is Emberpup Good?

Emberpup's highest base attribute is ATK, which shapes when you'd field it: a damage-first opener that wants early dodge-counters to snowball. The 371 total is starter-tier by design — the point of the line is what it becomes as a [Flameruff](/creatures/aniimo-flameruff/).
