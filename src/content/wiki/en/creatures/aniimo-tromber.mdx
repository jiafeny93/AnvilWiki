## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Tromber's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Tromber rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Tromber on a line with [Chirpi](/creatures/aniimo-chirpi/) (stage 1) → **Tromber** (stage 3) → [Cornet](/creatures/aniimo-cornet/) (stage 3) → [Tubster](/creatures/aniimo-tubster/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Tromber in Aniimo?

Tromber is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Tromber in Aniimo?

Tromber appears in the wild in its listed habitats (The Argent Strait), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Chirpi. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Tromber evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Tromber to Chirpi, Cornet, Tubster. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Tromber a good Aniimo to use?

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Tromber fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
