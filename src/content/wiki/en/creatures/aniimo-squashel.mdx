## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Squashel's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Squashel with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Squashel on a line with [Squarrel](/creatures/aniimo-squarrel/) (stage 1) → **Squashel** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
