## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Nimbi's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Nimbi rewards players who read the fight.

The official evolution tree places Nimbi on a line with **Nimbi** (stage 1) → [Dreaple](/creatures/aniimo-dreaple/) (stage 3) → [Turbo](/creatures/aniimo-turbo/) (stage 3). How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
