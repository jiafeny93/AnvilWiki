## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Nimbi's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Nimbi rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Nimbi on a line with **Nimbi** (stage 1) → [Dreaple](/creatures/aniimo-dreaple/) (stage 3) → [Turbo](/creatures/aniimo-turbo/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Nimbi Forms: Basic, Cloudmist, Rainstorm, Plateau

The official Aniimo Index gives Nimbi four separate form entries — and it is the roster's showcase for how forms work:

| Form | Element | Habitat | Base stats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Form** | Wind | Nimbus Fields, The Argent Strait | 442 |
| **Cloudmist Form** | Wind | Nimbus Fields | 442 |
| **Rainstorm Form** | Wind + Lightning | Nimbus Fields | 442 |
| **Plateau Form** | Wind + Ice | Driftwise Meadow | 442 |

Three things the official numbers make clear: every form **shares the exact same statline** (442 total, same spread, same [Shrouded in Mist] trait), forms **can add a second element** — Rainstorm conducts Lightning, Plateau carries Ice — and a form **can relocate** its spawn (Plateau appears around Driftwise Meadow instead of the Nimbus Fields). Cloudmist keeps pure Wind; its wool takes a golden tinge from preferring Sunny Daisy over grass, per community descriptions.

How each form is obtained — and how form-switching interacts with the Morph Orb system — is laid out in the [Aniimo forms guide](/guides/aniimo-forms/). Collecting all of a creature's forms also pays a completion bonus on its Aniilog entry; Nimbi's is +3 Constitution with a "Collect 4 Forms" research task.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Nimbi in Aniimo?

Nimbi is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Nimbi in Aniimo?

Nimbi appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Nimbus Fields, The Argent Strait), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Nimbi evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Nimbi to Dreaple, Turbo. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Nimbi a good Aniimo to use?

With REGEN as its strongest base attribute, Nimbi fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.

### Does Nimbi have a Cloudmist Form in Aniimo?

Yes — the official Aniimo Index lists Nimbi in four forms. Basic, Cloudmist, Rainstorm and Plateau. Rainstorm adds a Lightning typing and Plateau adds Ice and appears around Driftwise Meadow, while Cloudmist keeps pure Wind. All four share the same base statline. How Cloudmist is obtained has not been published yet.

### Do Nimbi forms change its stats?

No. The official index publishes every Nimbi form with the same base stats (442 total) and the same Shrouded in Mist trait — forms change appearance, element typing and sometimes habitat, not numbers.
