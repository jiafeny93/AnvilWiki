## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Flutternym's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Healer tag means its kit keeps the squad alive through sustain and recovery skills, freeing your other Aniimo slots for pure offense.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Flutternym on a line with **Flutternym** (stage 1) → [Gracewing](/creatures/aniimo-gracewing/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions
