Eko is Aniimo No.032, a Wind Aniimo listed as a Support in the official Aniimo Index — and one of the more interesting sheets in the mid-roster: a support whose attack stat outranks most attackers, a sonar kit that fights like a control mage, and an evolution line that goes all-in on the same game plan. This page rounds up everything confirmed about it ahead of the September 16, 2026 launch, checked against the official index and re-verified once servers open.

| 70 | 112 | 70 | 57 | 42 | 89 | **440** |

Six attributes sum to the total shown on the official index; ATK covers physical attack while BREAK scales guard damage. That 112 ATK on a Support is the headline — the kit spends it on debuff delivery, not damage charts. These are launch-preview base numbers — [inherit](/guides/aniimo-inherit-system/) building and equipment will move the final values.

## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Eko's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Eko rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Eko at the head of the line with **Eko** (stage 1) → [Eklue](/creatures/aniimo-eklue/) (stage 3). Eklue keeps the same Judgment Storm trait and Forest of Falling Stars habitat but brings 518 total stats, an upgraded sonar kit, and an innate that marks *everything* in view range. If the debuffer playstyle fits you, the whole line is built around it.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Judgment Storm:** When a skill applies a debuff to a target, Wind damage for the whole team increases by 20% for 15s.

**Combat skills:**

| Skill | Cost | Power |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Sonar Attack | 20 | 30 |
| Ice Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |
| Water Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |
| Arc Strike | 10 | 20 |

**Innate skill:** **ATK**.

The loop: Sonar Attack hits ten times in a cone and finishes by applying **Weakness Mark** (−7% Crit Resistance); the arc strikes then stun any marked enemy for 1.5 seconds. Note the skill list runs Ice and Water variants on a Wind-typed creature — skill elements and creature typing are separate axes in Aniimo. Skill costs are the resource spend shown on the official sheet; power is its listed base value. For how skills interact with the Twine system, see the [combat guide](/guides/aniimo-combat/).

## Where to Find Eko

The official index lists Eko in **Forest of Falling Stars**. Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

## Is Eko Good?

Eko's kit is a force multiplier — every mark it lands stuns, debuffs, and feeds Judgment Storm's team-wide Wind buff. Squads running Wind damage ([Nimbi](/creatures/aniimo-nimbi/), [Tromber](/creatures/aniimo-tromber/)) get the most out of it. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Eko in Aniimo?

Eko is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### Does Eko evolve in Aniimo?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Eko (stage 1) to Eklue (stage 3). The stage-3 form nearly doubles down on the same sonar-and-debuff identity with higher stats and a stronger Weakness Mark kit.

### Why does a Support Aniimo have 112 ATK?

Eko's official sheet lists ATK 112 against a 440 total — unusually high for the Support role. Its kit converts that attack into debuff delivery rather than raw damage output, and the Judgment Storm trait turns every debuff landed into a team-wide Wind damage buff.

### Where does Eko spawn in Aniimo?

The official index lists Eko in the Forest of Falling Stars, and it can fly at Fly Lv.1. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### What does Weakness Mark do in Aniimo?

Weakness Mark is the debuff Eko's kit applies — it lowers the target's Crit Resistance, and Eko's arc-strike skills stun enemies that are marked. Landing marks is also what procs the Judgment Storm team buff.
