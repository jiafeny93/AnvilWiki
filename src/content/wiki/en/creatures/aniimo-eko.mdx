## What Is the Aniimo Eko?

Eko is one of the many magical creatures—collectively known as Aniimo—that players will encounter on the main continent of Idyll. Developed by Pawprint Studio, Aniimo sets itself apart from traditional creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) games by blending open-world action RPG mechanics with deep companion bonding. Eko fits perfectly into this ecosystem as a highly agile, reactive companion.

In the game's narrative, players take on the role of a "Pathfinder." As you explore Idyll, your goal is to forge lasting bonds with these creatures using the Twine system. The Aniimo Eko has been highlighted in early promotional materials and beta showcases as a creature that excels in both exploration and dynamic, real-time combat encounters. While its exact elemental typing remains a subject of speculation until the full game drops, its visual design and the abilities it has demonstrated suggest it serves as a versatile, fast-paced companion for Pathfinders looking for a highly mobile playstyle.

Eko's swift movement patterns make it equally useful for traversing the difficult terrain of Idyll, potentially allowing Pathfinders to reach hidden areas or escape dangerous environmental hazards.

5. **Team Integration:** Once the Twine bond is secured, Eko will join your party, allowing you to swap to it in combat and utilize its unique abilities.
