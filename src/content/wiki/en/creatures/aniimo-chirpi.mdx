## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Chirpi's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Chirpi rewards players who read the fight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Chirpi in Aniimo?

Chirpi is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.
