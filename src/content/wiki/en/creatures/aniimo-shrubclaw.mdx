## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Shrubclaw's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts). Shrubclaw carries a dual Earth/Grass typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Shrubclaw with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Shrubclaw on a line with [Budclaw](/creatures/aniimo-budclaw/) (stage 1) → [Geoclaw](/creatures/aniimo-geoclaw/) (stage 3) → **Shrubclaw** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Shrubclaw in Aniimo?

Shrubclaw is a Earth/Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Shrubclaw in Aniimo?

Shrubclaw appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Berylline Causeway), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Budclaw. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Shrubclaw evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Shrubclaw to Budclaw, Geoclaw. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Shrubclaw a good Aniimo to use?

With physical defense as its strongest base attribute, Shrubclaw fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
