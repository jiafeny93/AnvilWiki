## Element and Role

As a Lightning creature, Dazmand's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Wind and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning, Ice and Earth. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Lightning (Electric in older posts).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Dazmand rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Dazmand on a line with [Pomegg](/creatures/aniimo-pomegg/) (stage 1) → **Dazmand** (stage 3) → [Pomawk](/creatures/aniimo-pomawk/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
