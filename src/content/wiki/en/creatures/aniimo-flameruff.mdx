## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Flameruff's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Flameruff on a line with [Emberpup](/creatures/aniimo-emberpup/) (stage 1) → **Flameruff** (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Flameruff in Aniimo?

Flameruff is a Fire Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Flameruff in Aniimo?

Flameruff appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Nimbus Fields, The Argent Strait, Echoback Landing, Beast Fang Ridge), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Emberpup. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Flameruff evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Flameruff to Emberpup. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Flameruff a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Flameruff fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
