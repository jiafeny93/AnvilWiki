## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Pomegg's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Pomegg with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.
