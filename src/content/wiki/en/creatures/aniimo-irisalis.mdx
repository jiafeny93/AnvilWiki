Irisalis is Aniimo's first legendary — a Grass DPS built around summoned flower clones, a self-revive, and one of the highest attack values seen in closed-beta data. Community databases list it at **No.013**, the exact slot the official Aniimo Index has published everything *except*: entries No.001–093 are live, and No.013 stays unrevealed. Everything on this page comes from the closed-beta Vain Crevice event and community databases; we will re-verify every number against the official index and in-game testing once servers open on September 16, 2026.

## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Irisalis's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag plus a clone-heavy kit means Irisalis fights like a one-creature strike team — you set up shadow clones, then let the whole field bloom at once. Details below.

## How to Get Irisalis: The Vain Crevice Event

Irisalis was not catchable in the wild in any beta test. It came exclusively from the **Vain Crevice** limited event, and the redemption route looked like this:

1. **Buy a Crevice Beacon** with Glimmer (the event currency) to start the encounter.
2. **Fight first.** The encounter opens against an Iris — real-time combat, chip its HP down.
3. **Then soothe.** After the fight phase, you calm the creature by offering antiipods and mega pods.
4. **Collect Pearl Cascades.** Each full clear awards 1–2 Pearl Cascades. Expect 25+ clears to stockpile enough.
5. **Redeem 99 Pearl Cascades** in the event shop for an Irisalis — and it arrives at **Perfect quality** (see our [quality tiers guide](/guides/aniimo-quality-system/) for what that means).

Two footnotes from beta data: each clear also had a reported ~0.5% chance to drop Irisalis directly, skipping the redemption, and the event required character level 20 to enter. Whether Vain Crevice returns at launch, and on what schedule, has not been announced — event-only legendaries usually rotate back, so watch the in-game event calendar after September 16, 2026.

## Skills and Battle Kit

Irisalis's kit is the most mechanically distinctive in the beta roster. Community beta footage confirms the following:

- **Florae Descent** — its headliner attack, listed at **240 Might**, the single largest power value seen on a Grass skill.
- **Clone summoning** — its signature mechanic. Iris Shot hits summon a shadow clone that fights alongside you for 30 seconds, upgradeable to 2 persistent clones; the ultimate is a phantom dive that leaves clones at the impact point, upgradeable to 2 plus 3 more.
- **Flower-beam bursts** — sprint-finishing or landing the final normal-attack segment triggers every active clone into a flower beam (15 Might each) — the payoff window for maintaining clones.
- **Dance Power synergy** — builds Dance Power stacks like the [Iris line](/creatures/aniimo-irisal/); at 9 stacks the next skill costs half EP, keeping the clone rotation running.
- **Self-revive** — once every 180 seconds, a fallen Irisalis dissolves into flowers and reblooms back into the fight.
- **Glide Lv.3** — mount-tier flight, the highest traversal tier in the current data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Irisalis the same creature as Irisal?

No. Irisal is Aniimo No.012, the stage-3 evolution of Iris available in the wild. Irisalis is a separate event legendary that community databases list at No.013 — the slot the official Aniimo Index has not yet revealed. Their official story connection, if any, has not been published.

### How do I get Irisalis in Aniimo?

Irisalis was reward-exclusive to the Vain Crevice limited event in the last closed beta. You buy a Crevice Beacon with Glimmer, fight and then soothe the encounter, earn 1-2 Pearl Cascades per clear, and redeem 99 of them in the event shop for a Perfect-quality Irisalis. Each clear also had a small reported chance to drop Irisalis directly.

### Can Irisalis be Sparkling (shiny)?

No. Datamined beta values give every Aniimo a Sparkling rate of about 1 in 20,000, with Irisalis the one exception — its rate is zero, so a shiny Irisalis cannot roll.

### What element is Irisalis?

Community beta databases list Irisalis as a Grass creature in the DPS role with a 550 total base statline — ATK 130 and REGEN 108 are the published values, with the remaining attributes to be confirmed at launch.

### Is Irisalis in the official Aniimo Index yet?

Not yet. The official Index currently publishes No.001-093 plus two teaser slots, and the No.013 position is unrevealed. Community data from the closed beta places Irisalis there.
