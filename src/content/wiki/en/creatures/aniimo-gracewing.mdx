## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Gracewing's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Healer tag means its kit keeps the squad alive through sustain and recovery skills, freeing your other Aniimo slots for pure offense.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Gracewing on a line with [Flutternym](/creatures/aniimo-flutternym/) (stage 1) → **Gracewing** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
