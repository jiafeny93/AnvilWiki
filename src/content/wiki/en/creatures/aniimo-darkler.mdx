Darkler is the Aniimo creature that *wasn't* — a shadowy ambusher the community expected for months, name-checked across early fan guides and tier lists, yet absent from the official Aniimo Index when the full 94-entry roster published ahead of the September 16, 2026 launch. If you searched "aniimo darkler" expecting a profile page, here is the honest one: what the name was, where it came from, and what — if anything — might still become of it.

> **Last updated: August 18, 2026**
> *Note: Aniimo's closed beta tests have concluded ahead of its confirmed September 16, 2026 global launch. This page tracks an unpublished community-era creature concept, not an official roster entry.*

## The Short Version

The official Aniimo Index publishes 94 creatures — No.001 through No.093 plus two teaser slots — every one of them with elements, roles, base stats, and skill sheets. **Darkler is not among them, under any numbering.** The name has never appeared on an official creature sheet, in the official wiki, or in any beta data dump the community has surfaced.

What you are looking at is a pre-launch community expectation that the official roster never cashed in.

## Where the Name Came From

Before the official index published, early Aniimo showcases and trailers were the only information source — and fans filled rosters by interpretation. In that era, "Darkler" circulated as the presumed dark-element ambusher: a stealth creature glimpsed (or believed to be glimpsed) in environment showcases, extrapolated into a full identity by guides like ours. Some of our own older pages still carry it with a Rumored tag for exactly this reason.

That era ended when the official Index went live with real data. Interpretation-based roster pages have been replaced across this site by the 94 official entries — see the [full creatures list](/creatures/aniimo-creatures-list/). Darkler is the one prominent community name the official roster left behind, which is why this page exists: searchers deserve the answer, not a 404.

## Could Darkler Still Show Up?

Three live possibilities, all unconfirmed:

1. **An unrevealed slot.** The official Index still hides entries (No.013 among them, with the event legendary [Irisalis](/creatures/aniimo-irisalis/) the community favorite for it). A future reveal could match what fans called Darkler.
2. **A rename.** Beta-to-launch renames are common in the genre; a published creature could already be "Darkler" under another name.
3. **Nothing.** The community guessed itself a creature, the way pre-launch communities do.

The launch on September 16, 2026 settles it either way — if a Darkler appears in the official index or the game, this page becomes a full profile the same day, with the [launch-day update plan](/release/aniimo-release-date/) covering when we re-verify the roster.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Darkler in Aniimo?

Not in the official published roster. The official Aniimo Index lists 94 creatures (No.001-093 plus two teaser slots) and Darkler is not one of them. The name comes from pre-launch community discussion and has never appeared on an official creature sheet.

### What was Darkler supposed to be?

A shadowy, stealth-themed creature from early community speculation — expected to be an ambusher with high burst damage and critical-hit potential, roaming dark biomes at night. None of that was ever confirmed by an official source, and this page keeps it clearly labeled as speculation.
