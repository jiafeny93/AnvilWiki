## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Dreaple's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Dreaple rewards players who read the fight.

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Counting Sheep:** Every 10s, the next skill applies 1 stack of [Drowsiness] to the target. At 3 stacks, the target enters [Nightmare] for 20s. Targets in [Nightmare] have their Dark Resistance reduced by 17% and cannot move or cast skills for 3s. Taking damage removes the control effect.
