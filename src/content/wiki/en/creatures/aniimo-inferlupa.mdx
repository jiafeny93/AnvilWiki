## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Inferlupa's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Inferlupa carries a dual Fire/Dark typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Inferlupa with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Inferlupa on a line with [Emberpup](/creatures/aniimo-emberpup/) (stage 1) → [Flameruff](/creatures/aniimo-flameruff/) (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
