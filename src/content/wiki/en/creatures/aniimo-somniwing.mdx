## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Somniwing's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Somniwing carries a dual Wind/Grass typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.
