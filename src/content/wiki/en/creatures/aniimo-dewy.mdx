## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Dewy's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Dewy rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Dewy on a line with **Dewy** (stage 1) → [Fragrancier](/creatures/aniimo-fragrancier/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
