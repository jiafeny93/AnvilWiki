## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Stellarys's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Stellarys on a line with [Celestis](/creatures/aniimo-celestis/) (stage 1) → **Stellarys** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Stellarys in Aniimo?

Stellarys is a Dark Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Stellarys in Aniimo?

Stellarys appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Forest of Falling Stars), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Celestis. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Stellarys evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Stellarys to Celestis. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Stellarys a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Stellarys fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
