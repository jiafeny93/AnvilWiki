## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Fragrancier's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Fragrancier rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Fragrancier on a line with [Dewy](/creatures/aniimo-dewy/) (stage 1) → **Fragrancier** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Fragrancier in Aniimo?

Fragrancier is a Dark Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Fragrancier in Aniimo?

Fragrancier appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Sea of Flowers), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Dewy. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Fragrancier evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Fragrancier to Dewy. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Fragrancier a good Aniimo to use?

With REGEN as its strongest base attribute, Fragrancier fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
