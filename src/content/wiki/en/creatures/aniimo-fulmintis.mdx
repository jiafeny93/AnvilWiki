## Element and Role

As a Lightning creature, Fulmintis's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Wind and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning, Ice and Earth. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Lightning (Electric in older posts).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Fulmintis on a line with [Wisptis](/creatures/aniimo-wisptis/) (stage 1) → **Fulmintis** (stage 3) → [Ignitis](/creatures/aniimo-ignitis/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Fulmintis in Aniimo?

Fulmintis is a Lightning Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.
