## Element and Role

As a Lightning creature, Bolty's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Wind and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning, Ice and Earth. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Lightning (Electric in older posts).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Bolty with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Bolty on a line with **Bolty** (stage 1) → [Blazen](/creatures/aniimo-blazen/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

The official index lists Bolty in **Blitzwood**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Blazen](/creatures/aniimo-blazen/).
