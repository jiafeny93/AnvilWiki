## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Infergon's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

The official evolution tree places Infergon on a line with [Helmwhelp](/creatures/aniimo-helmwhelp/) (stage 2) → [Helgon](/creatures/aniimo-helgon/) (stage 3) → **Infergon** (stage 3).
