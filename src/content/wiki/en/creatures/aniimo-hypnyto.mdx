Hypnyto is one of the named arrivals of the **Whisperwake Isles** update — Aniimo's first major content drop, live since October 29, 2026 as Season 1 Part 2. The official update notice confirms its name, its delivery route and — for some of the launch roster — its element and role. What it does not have yet is an entry in the official Aniimo index with stats and skills, so this page tracks exactly what is confirmed and what stays TBA. The full launch roster is on our [Whisperwake Isles guide](/guides/aniimo-whisperwake-isles/).

## What We Know So Far

Hypnyto is the only one of the launch-named Aniimo delivered through the Companion Handbook, and the official notice confirms both a base form and a Prismana Hypnyto form. That makes it the season's guaranteed companion pick — no event RNG required, just handbook progression.
