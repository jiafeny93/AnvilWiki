## Element and Role

As a Lightning creature, Veilfloat's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Wind and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning, Ice and Earth. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Lightning (Electric in older posts).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Veilfloat rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Veilfloat on a line with [Bulbly](/creatures/aniimo-bulbly/) (stage 1) → **Veilfloat** (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Veilfloat in Aniimo?

Veilfloat is a Lightning Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Veilfloat in Aniimo?

Veilfloat appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Tideblossom Coast), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system.
