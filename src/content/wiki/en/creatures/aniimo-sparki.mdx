## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Sparki's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Sparki on a line with **Sparki** (stage 1) → [Flamerion](/creatures/aniimo-flamerion/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

The official index lists Sparki in **Nimbus Fields**, **Beast Fang Ridge**, **Blitzwood**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Pranky](/creatures/aniimo-pranky/).
