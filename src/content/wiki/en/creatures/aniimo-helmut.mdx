Six attributes sum to the total shown on the official index; ATK covers physical attack while BREAK scales guard damage. The shape tells the story — BREAK 60 against ATK 52 is a statline that cracks guards rather than chases damage charts, and P.DEF 68 anchors it through the counterattacks that come with standing in melee. These are launch-preview base numbers — [inherit](/guides/aniimo-inherit-system/) building and equipment will move the final values.

## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Helmut's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag is the role to understand here: BREAK is the attribute that scales guard damage, and Breaker kits exist to empty guard gauges. A squad around Helmut plays stun-and-stagger — it cracks the target open, the DPS cashes the window.

## Evolution Line (It Branches)

The official evolution tree places Helmut at the head of a **branch**: **Helmut** (stage 1) → [Pawney](/creatures/aniimo-pawney/) (stage 3) *or* [Rookey](/creatures/aniimo-rookey/) (stage 3). Two stage-3 destinations from one base form is a rarer structure in the published roster — worth deciding early which final form fits your squad before investing.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Helmut in Aniimo?

Helmut is a Dark Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### What does the Breaker role do in Aniimo?

Breaker kits specialize in BREAK — the attribute that scales guard damage. While DPS creatures drain health bars, Breakers crack guard gauges, opening stagger windows for the rest of the squad to unload. Helmut's kit is built entirely around that job.

### Does Helmut evolve in Aniimo?

Yes — and it branches. The official evolution tree links Helmut (stage 1) to both Pawney (stage 3) and Rookey (stage 3), a two-way split rather than a straight line. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Where does Helmut spawn in Aniimo?
