## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Cornet's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Cornet on a line with [Chirpi](/creatures/aniimo-chirpi/) (stage 1) → [Tromber](/creatures/aniimo-tromber/) (stage 3) → **Cornet** (stage 3) → [Tubster](/creatures/aniimo-tubster/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Cornet in Aniimo?

Cornet is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Cornet in Aniimo?

Cornet appears in the wild in its listed habitats (The Argent Strait), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Chirpi. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Cornet evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Cornet to Chirpi, Tromber, Tubster. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Cornet a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Cornet fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
