Per that announcement, Pathfinders team up to take on the new boss challenges — co-op group content, not standard wild encounters. Like the beta's other new names ([Mophling](/creatures/aniimo-mophling/), [Sausite](/creatures/aniimo-sausite/)), Bubbask hasn't received an official Index entry or stat sheet yet, so this page tracks what's confirmed, what's TBA, and where guessing starts.

## Where the Name Came From

Bubbask was named in the official FunPlus press release announcing the Global Closed Beta: "Pathfinders can team up and take on the new boss challenges that await them: Bubbask and Coraliz." That's the entire official record so far — no location, no mechanics, no stat sheet. Press coverage of the beta repeated the names without adding detail.

One honest caveat: "Bubbask" has not appeared in the official 94-creature Index, and bosses are a different category from the catchable roster. Until launch, treat any Bubbask stat table, element claim, or "how to beat Bubbask" guide you see elsewhere as fabricated — there is nothing official to build one from.
