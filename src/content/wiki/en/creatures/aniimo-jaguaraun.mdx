Jaguaraun is one of the named arrivals of the **Whisperwake Isles** update — Aniimo's first major content drop, live since October 29, 2026 as Season 1 Part 2. The official update notice confirms its name, its delivery route and — for some of the launch roster — its element and role. What it does not have yet is an entry in the official Aniimo index with stats and skills, so this page tracks exactly what is confirmed and what stays TBA. The full launch roster is on our [Whisperwake Isles guide](/guides/aniimo-whisperwake-isles/).

## What We Know So Far

Jaguaraun is the update's co-created Aniimo — a design the community helped shape. Its Fantasy Resonance event starts three weeks after launch, so it is the last of the named arrivals to become obtainable.

The stat sheet lands when the official index publishes it — we update this page the day it does, and Jaguaraun enters the [tier list](/tier-list/aniimo-tier-list/) rankings at the same time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Jaguaraun an official Aniimo creature?

Yes. Jaguaraun is named in the official Season 1 Part 2 update notice dated October 27, 2026 — the Whisperwake Isles launch announcement. It is official — it just has no published stat sheet yet.

### How do I get Jaguaraun in Aniimo?

Fantasy Resonance event, November 20 – December 10, 2026 — Event reward — not a wild spawn. The official notice lists no stat sheet for it yet.

### What element is Jaguaraun?

Jaguaraun is officially listed as Lightning element, Breaker role. Everything else — skills, base stats, evolution — is TBA until the official index publishes its sheet.

### Does Jaguaraun have a Prismana form?

Not confirmed. The official notice does not list a Prismana form for Jaguaraun — its Fantasy Resonance event is the confirmed delivery.

### Is Jaguaraun good? Where does it rank?

Nobody can honestly rank Jaguaraun yet — the official stat sheet has not published. It joins the [tier list](/tier-list/aniimo-tier-list/) the day official data lands; until then any ranking you see elsewhere is speculation.
