## Where the Name Came From

Sausite entered the record through the official FunPlus press release of June 8, 2026 announcing the Global Closed Beta, which listed the test's new Aniimo as Bubbeep, Popapus, Mophling, and Sausite. The spelling variation comes from later press coverage — at least one major outlet wrote "Saucite" — but the official spelling has been consistent in FunPlus and Aniimo channel materials.

That makes Sausite an official creature awaiting official data, a different case from community-era names like [Darkler](/creatures/aniimo-darkler/) that never appeared in an official source at all.
