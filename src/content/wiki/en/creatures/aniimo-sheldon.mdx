## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Sheldon's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Sheldon on a line with [Shelly](/creatures/aniimo-shelly/) (stage 1) → **Sheldon** (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Where to Find Sheldon

The official index lists Sheldon in **Echoback Landing**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Emberpup](/creatures/aniimo-emberpup/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Sheldon in Aniimo?

Sheldon is a Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Sheldon in Aniimo?

Sheldon appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Echoback Landing), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Shelly. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

Yes — the official evolution tree links Sheldon to Shelly. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Sheldon fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
