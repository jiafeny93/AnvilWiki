## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Bailite's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Bailite rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Bailite in Aniimo?

Bailite is a Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Bailite in Aniimo?

Bailite appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Zephyrus Landbridge), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Cozite. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Bailite evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Bailite to Cozite. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Bailite a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Bailite fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
