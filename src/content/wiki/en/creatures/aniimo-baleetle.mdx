## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Baleetle's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Baleetle on a line with **Baleetle** (stage 1) → [Waleetle](/creatures/aniimo-waleetle/) (stage 3) → [Bouldus](/creatures/aniimo-bouldus/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Where to Find Baleetle

The official index lists Baleetle in **Berylline Causeway**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Budclaw](/creatures/aniimo-budclaw/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

Building around the Earth element? [Bouldus](/creatures/aniimo-bouldus/) is another confirmed Earth pick.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Baleetle in Aniimo?

Baleetle is a Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

Baleetle appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Berylline Causeway), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.
