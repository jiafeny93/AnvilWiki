## Element and Role

As a Wind creature, Helgon's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Dark and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Helgon with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Helgon in Aniimo?

Helgon is a Wind Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Helgon in Aniimo?

Helgon appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Russet Highlands), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Helmwhelp. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Helgon evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Helgon to Helmwhelp, Infergon. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Helgon a good Aniimo to use?

With physical defense as its strongest base attribute, Helgon fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
