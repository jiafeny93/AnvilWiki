## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Bouldus's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Bouldus rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Bouldus on a line with [Baleetle](/creatures/aniimo-baleetle/) (stage 1) → [Waleetle](/creatures/aniimo-waleetle/) (stage 3) → **Bouldus** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Bouldus in Aniimo?

Bouldus is a Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Bouldus in Aniimo?

Bouldus appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Berylline Causeway), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Baleetle. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Bouldus evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Bouldus to Baleetle, Waleetle. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Bouldus a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Bouldus fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
