## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Budclaw's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts). Budclaw carries a dual Earth/Grass typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Budclaw with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Budclaw in Aniimo?

Budclaw is a Earth/Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

Budclaw appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Zephyrus Landbridge, Nimbus Fields, Berylline Causeway, Beast Fang Ridge), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Budclaw evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Budclaw to Geoclaw, Shrubclaw. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.
