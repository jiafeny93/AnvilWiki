## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Pebbling's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Pebbling with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.
