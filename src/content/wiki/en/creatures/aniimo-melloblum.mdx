## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Melloblum's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Melloblum rewards players who read the fight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Melloblum in Aniimo?

Melloblum is a Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Support role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Melloblum in Aniimo?

Melloblum appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Rosetower Woods), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Budsquire. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Melloblum evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Melloblum to Budsquire, Thornblade. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Melloblum a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Melloblum fits the Support role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
