## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Flamerion's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Evolution Line

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Flamerion in Aniimo?

Flamerion is a Fire Aniimo in the official index, filling the Regen role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Flamerion in Aniimo?

Flamerion appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Beast Fang Ridge), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Sparki. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.
