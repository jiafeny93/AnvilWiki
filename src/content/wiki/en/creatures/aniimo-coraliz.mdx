## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Coraliz's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts). Coraliz carries a dual Earth/Water typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Coraliz in Aniimo?

Coraliz is a Earth/Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the Regen role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Coraliz in Aniimo?

Coraliz appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Crescent Bay), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Reefish. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Coraliz evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Coraliz to Reefish. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

With REGEN as its strongest base attribute, Coraliz fits the Regen role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
