## Element and Role

As a Lightning creature, Besauce's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Wind and are resisted to 0.625x by Lightning, Ice and Earth. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Lightning (Electric in older posts).

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.
