## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Grizbo's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Grizbo on a line with [Cubbo](/creatures/aniimo-cubbo/) (stage 1) → **Grizbo** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Raging Rampage:** Builds [Rage] during battle. Basic attacks grant 2 points, every 10 EP spent on skills grants 10, and taking damage grants 2. At 100 points, enters the [Enraged] state.[Enraged] increases ATK Speed and own skill damage by 30%, increases the EP recovery rate by 25%, and prevents [Rage] gain. Lasts 20s.

## Where to Find Grizbo

The official index lists Grizbo in **Rosetower Woods**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Pomawk](/creatures/aniimo-pomawk/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Grizbo in Aniimo?

Grizbo is a Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Grizbo in Aniimo?

Grizbo appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Rosetower Woods), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Cubbo. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Grizbo evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Grizbo to Cubbo. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Grizbo a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Grizbo fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
