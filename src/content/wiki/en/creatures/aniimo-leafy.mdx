## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Leafy's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Leafy carries a dual Grass/Water typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Leafy on a line with [Skippy](/creatures/aniimo-skippy/) (stage 1) → [Pranky](/creatures/aniimo-pranky/) (stage 2). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Leafy in Aniimo?

Leafy is a Grass/Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the Regen role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Leafy in Aniimo?

Leafy appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Mistwoods), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Skippy. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Leafy evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Leafy to Skippy, Pranky. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Leafy a good Aniimo to use?

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Leafy fits the Regen role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
