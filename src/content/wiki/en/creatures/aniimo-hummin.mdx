## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Hummin's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Hummin with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Hummin on a line with **Hummin** (stage 1) → [Witchin](/creatures/aniimo-witchin/) (stage 3) → [Tuckin](/creatures/aniimo-tuckin/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Hummin in Aniimo?

Hummin is a Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Hummin in Aniimo?

Hummin appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Nimbus Fields, Mistwoods, The Argent Strait, Forest of Falling Stars, Blitzwood), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Hummin evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Hummin to Witchin, Tuckin. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Hummin fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
