## Element and Role

As a Grass creature, Thornblade's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Grass and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Thornblade on a line with [Budsquire](/creatures/aniimo-budsquire/) (stage 1) → **Thornblade** (stage 3) → [Melloblum](/creatures/aniimo-melloblum/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Thornblade in Aniimo?

Thornblade is a Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Thornblade in Aniimo?

Thornblade appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Sea of Flowers), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Budsquire. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Thornblade evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Thornblade to Budsquire, Melloblum. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Thornblade a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Thornblade fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
