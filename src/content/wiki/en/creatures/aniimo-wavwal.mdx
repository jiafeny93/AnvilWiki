## Element and Role

As an Ice creature, Wavwal's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Water and Lightning and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Ice, Earth and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Wavwal with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Wavwal on a line with [Cheekie](/creatures/aniimo-cheekie/) (stage 1) → **Wavwal** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Wavwal in Aniimo?

Wavwal is a Ice Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Wavwal in Aniimo?

Wavwal appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Crescent Bay), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Cheekie. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Wavwal evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Wavwal to Cheekie. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Wavwal a good Aniimo to use?

With BREAK as its strongest base attribute, Wavwal fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
