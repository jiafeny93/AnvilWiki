## Element and Role

As a Fire creature, Magmarex's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Grass and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Fire, Water, Earth and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Magmarex carries a dual Fire/Earth typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Breaker tag marks a kit focused on breaking enemy guards and stagger bars. Teams pair a Breaker like Magmarex with a DPS who benefits from openings it creates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Magmarex in Aniimo?

Magmarex is a Fire/Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the Breaker role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Magmarex in Aniimo?

Magmarex appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Zephyrus Landbridge), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Pebbling. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Magmarex evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Magmarex to Pebbling, Lavazar, Geodeback. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Magmarex a good Aniimo to use?

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Magmarex fits the Breaker role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
