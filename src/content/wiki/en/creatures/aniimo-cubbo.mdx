## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Cubbo's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Cubbo on a line with **Cubbo** (stage 1) → [Grizbo](/creatures/aniimo-grizbo/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

At 100 points, enters the [Enraged] state.[Enraged] increases ATK Speed and own skill damage by 30%, increases the EP recovery rate by 25%, and prevents [Rage] gain. Lasts 20s.

## Where to Find Cubbo

The official index lists Cubbo in **Rosetower Woods**. Other Aniimo sharing that ground include [Grizbo](/creatures/aniimo-grizbo/). Spawn points, timers, and Twine-bond strategies go into our [catching guide](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) — the short version: weaken it in real-time combat, then bond rather than trap.

Building around the Earth element? [Cozite](/creatures/aniimo-cozite/) is another confirmed Earth pick.
