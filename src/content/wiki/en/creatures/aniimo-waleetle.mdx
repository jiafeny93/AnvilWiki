## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Waleetle's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Waleetle on a line with [Baleetle](/creatures/aniimo-baleetle/) (stage 1) → **Waleetle** (stage 3) → [Bouldus](/creatures/aniimo-bouldus/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Waleetle in Aniimo?

Waleetle is a Earth Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Waleetle in Aniimo?

Waleetle appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Berylline Causeway), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Baleetle. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Waleetle evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Waleetle to Baleetle, Bouldus. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Waleetle a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Waleetle fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
