## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Cozite's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts).

The Support tag means its kit amplifies the team instead of topping the damage chart — debuffs, marks, and utility that make everyone else hit harder. Cozite rewards players who read the fight.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Cozite on a line with **Cozite** (stage 1) → [Bailite](/creatures/aniimo-bailite/) (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).
