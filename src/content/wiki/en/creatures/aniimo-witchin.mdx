## Element and Role

As a Dark creature, Witchin's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire, Grass and Light and are resisted to 0.625x by Water and Wind. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Witchin carries a dual Dark/Grass typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.

The official evolution tree places Witchin on a line with [Hummin](/creatures/aniimo-hummin/) (stage 1) → **Witchin** (stage 3) → [Tuckin](/creatures/aniimo-tuckin/) (stage 3). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Witchin in Aniimo?

Witchin is a Dark/Grass Aniimo in the official index, filling the Regen role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Witchin in Aniimo?

Witchin appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Mistwoods), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Hummin. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Witchin evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Witchin to Hummin, Tuckin. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

With HP as its strongest base attribute, Witchin fits the Regen role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
