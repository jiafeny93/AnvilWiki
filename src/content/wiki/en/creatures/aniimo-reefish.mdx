## Element and Role

As an Earth creature, Reefish's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Lightning and Ice and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Earth and Dark. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/). Older community posts may call this element by another name: Earth (Rock in older posts). Reefish carries a dual Earth/Water typing, so both matchups apply — a real advantage for coverage on one roster slot.

The Regen tag covers kits built around recovery and sustained attrition — outlasting opponents rather than bursting them down.
