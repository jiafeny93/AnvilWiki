## Element and Role

As a Water creature, Gachapus's attacks deal 1.6x damage into Fire and Earth and are resisted to 0.625x by Water, Grass, Ice and Light. Aniimo has no immunities, and same-element hits are always resisted — the full grid is on our [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/).

The DPS tag means its kit is built to deal the bulk of its team's damage. In practice you Twine into it during damage windows and swap out when you need defense or healing.

## Evolution Line

The official evolution tree places Gachapus on a line with [Popapus](/creatures/aniimo-popapus/) (stage 1) → **Gachapus** (stage 3). Each stage raises the stat ceiling and unlocks stronger skill variants, so base-form investment carries forward through the family.

How bonding and stage-ups actually play out in-game is covered in our [evolution guide](/creatures/aniimo-evolution/).

## Skills and Trait

- **Trait — Gachapon:** Each basic attack and skill triggers the gacha once, randomly firing an orange, purple, blue, or gold bubble and adding that bubble color to the gacha slots. Gold bubbles have a 1% chance to appear, while each other color has a 33% chance to appear. When the slots hold 5 bubbles of the same color, or when a gold bubble appears, all bubbles are cleared and [Jackpot] begins for 10s. In [Jackpot], basic attacks and skills no longer trigger the gacha and always fire Rainbow Bubbles. Basic attack damage +60%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What element is Gachapus in Aniimo?

Gachapus is a Water Aniimo in the official index, filling the DPS role. Its matchups follow Aniimo's 1.6x super-effective / 0.625x resisted system, detailed on our type chart page.

### How do I get Gachapus in Aniimo?

Gachapus appears in the wild in its listed habitats (Crescent Bay), where you weaken it through real-time combat and then bond with it using the Twine system. As a later stage of its line, it can also be reached by evolving Popapus. Exact spawn maps will be added after the September 16, 2026 launch.

### Does Gachapus evolve?

Yes — the official evolution tree links Gachapus to Popapus. Stage-ups raise stats and unlock stronger skill variants.

### Is Gachapus a good Aniimo to use?

With ATK as its strongest base attribute, Gachapus fits the DPS role best. Its final tier standing depends on the launch meta — see our tier list for current rankings.
