## Where the Name Came From

Mophling entered the record the clean way: an official FunPlus press release on June 8, 2026, announcing the Global Closed Beta, listed the "new Aniimo introduced" for the test as Bubbeep, Popapus, Mophling, and Sausite. Press coverage of the beta in July 2026 repeated the roster. There is no ambiguity about whether Mophling is real — the open question is only what it is.

That makes Mophling a different case from community-era names like [Darkler](/creatures/aniimo-darkler/), which never appeared in an official source at all. Mophling is an official creature awaiting its official data.
