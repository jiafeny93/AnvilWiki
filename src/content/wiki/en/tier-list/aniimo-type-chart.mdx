Understanding the **aniimo [type chart](/tier-list/type-chart/)** is the absolute foundation of mastering combat in Pawprint Studio's upcoming open-world action RPG. Unlike traditional turn-based creature collectors, Aniimo blends real-time ARPG mechanics with elemental affinities, meaning that knowing your strengths and weaknesses is vital for dodging, countering, and surviving. As a Pathfinder adventuring across the vast continent of Idyll, you will bond with magical creatures using the unique "Twine" system. To make the most of these synergies, you need to know exactly how different elements interact. While the game is still in development and exact matchup multipliers are subject to change, we have compiled everything revealed so far about elemental resistances, vulnerabilities, and combat strategy to give you a massive head start.

## How the Aniimo Type Chart Works in Real-Time Combat

In many classic creature-[catching](/creatures/aniimo-catching/) games, the **aniimo type chart** dictates a strict rock-paper-scissors turn-based system. Aniimo flips this script by integrating elemental mechanics directly into a fast-paced, real-time action RPG. When you bond with a creature using the Twine system, you don't just give commands—you see through their eyes and wield their abilities directly.

Because combat happens in real-time, type effectiveness isn't just about dealing extra damage; it dictates combat flow. Exploiting an enemy's elemental weakness in Aniimo is expected to stagger them, break guards, or create localized status effects in the open world (such as using a water-type ability to douse a fire-type barrier). Positioning, active dodging, and rapid swapping between your active Aniimo are just as critical as the types you bring into a fight. A deep understanding of elemental matchups allows Pathfinders to survive against towering bosses that would otherwise wipe an unprepared team.

## Advanced Combat Strategies Using the Type Chart

Simply knowing the **aniimo type chart** won't be enough to conquer the toughest fantasy quests and [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) raids in Idyll. Because Aniimo features co-op multiplayer and action-heavy mechanics, you must leverage the Twine system to its absolute limit. 

### 1. The Hit-and-Run Elemental Swap
Because you can cycle through your bonded Aniimo in real-time, the optimal strategy involves striking an enemy's weakness, then immediately swapping to a resistant type to absorb the boss's inevitable counter-attack. If a boss readies a massive Fire attack, swap to a Water or Earth-type Aniimo to mitigate the damage, then swap back to your Fire or Lightning DPS to continue the assault.

### 2. Environmental Elemental Triggers
The open world of Idyll is highly interactive. Based on gameplay reveals, elements aren't restricted to the creatures. You can use the environment to your advantage. Luring a heavy Earth-type boss into a body of water, then unleashing a Lightning-type Aniimo ability, is expected to deal massive area-of-effect bonus damage. Exploration and spatial awareness are just as important as your loadout.

### 3. Co-Op Twine Synergies
When playing multiplayer co-op with other Pathfinders, team composition and the type chart overlap brilliantly. One player can use a Nature or Water Aniimo to apply defensive buffs or trap enemies, while another player coordinates a devastating Fire or Lightning combo. This emphasizes the game's focus on collaborative strategy over simple brute force.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Aniimo have a traditional type chart like other creature collectors?

Yes, Aniimo features a comprehensive type chart, but it is adapted for real-time action RPG combat. Instead of turn-based multipliers, exploiting elemental weaknesses staggers enemies, creates combo opportunities, and interacts directly with the open-world environment of Idyll.

### How many elemental types are in Aniimo?

Aniimo has 9 elements: Fire, Water, Grass, Lightning, Ice, Earth, Wind, Light, and Dark — confirmed by the official wiki's element filter. Light, Earth and Lightning appear as Holy, Rock and Electric in older references; they are the same elements.

### What is the best starter Aniimo for elemental matchups?

Based on current reveals, Emberpup is a highly aggressive Fire-type that excels in the early game, but the ultimate best choice depends on your preferred ARPG playstyle. Players who prefer defense might opt for a bruiser like Eklue, while those who like tactical combat might lean toward control creatures like Eko.

### Does the Twine system affect type advantages?

Yes, the Twine bonding system is central to combat. When you bond with an Aniimo, you take on their elemental affinities, resistances, and weaknesses. This means your personal dodging and aiming skills combined with your active creature's typing determine whether you win or lose a matchup.

### Will type effectiveness matter in Aniimo's multiplayer co-op mode?

Absolutely. Type effectiveness will be crucial in co-op multiplayer quests. Players will need to coordinate their elemental abilities to trigger devastating synergies—like combining Wind and Fire for massive damage—while ensuring their team has the right resistances to survive powerful world bosses.

### Can Aniimo have dual typings?

While not officially confirmed for every creature, the game's deep combat system and the presence of hybrid-style creatures like Eklue strongly suggest that dual typings will exist. Having dual types allows for more complex team building and broader coverage of the type chart.
