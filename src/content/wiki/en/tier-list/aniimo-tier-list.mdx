## How to Build the Best Team in Aniimo

Creating the best team goes beyond simply picking S-tier companions from an Aniimo tier list. Because you can seamlessly switch between your bonded creatures in real-time combat, building a balanced roster is essential for overcoming the challenges on the continent of Idyll. 

### Core Combat Roles

1. **Main DPS:** Your primary damage dealer (like Eko or Emberpup). Use the Twine system to directly control their abilities and dodge enemy attacks.
2. **Tank/Utility:** A durable companion (like Helmut) to absorb heavy hits or draw aggro during difficult boss fights.
3. **Support/Healer:** A creature focused on restoring your team's health or applying buffs, which is incredibly valuable in the game's co-op [multiplayer](/guides/aniimo-multiplayer/) fantasy quests.

### Elemental Coverage

Aniimo features elemental mechanics that exploit enemy weaknesses. While the exact elemental chart is still being finalized, maintaining a team with diverse elemental types ensures you can adapt to any open-world encounter. Swapping creatures mid-combat using the Twine system is expected to be a DPS-enhancing mechanic, rewarding players who master elemental combos.

## Twine System: Maximizing Your Creature's Potential

The defining feature of Aniimo is the Twine bonding system. Unlike traditional creature-catching games where you merely issue commands, Twine allows you, the Pathfinder, to see through your companion's eyes and wield their abilities directly. 

When evaluating creatures for this tier list, we heavily weigh how effectively their kit performs in this direct-control action format. For example, a creature with sluggish animations might rank lower in an RPG setting, but if its abilities flow perfectly into an action-combo string via Twine, its tier placement skyrockets. As you progress, focus on upgrading your bond level with S and A-tier companions to unlock their full potential, enhancing their real-time combat responsiveness.

## Co-op Strategy: How Tiers Shift in Multiplayer

Aniimo will feature co-op and multiplayer fantasy quests, which dramatically shifts how you should view a companion's value. A creature sitting in the B-tier for solo open-world exploration might instantly become S-tier when playing with friends.

For instance, Eklue (the support creature) may struggle to take down a massive world boss on its own. However, when paired with a dedicated tank like Helmut holding the enemy's attention, Eklue's healing and buffs can keep the entire squad alive, making it an invaluable multiplayer asset. Always consider your playstyle—if you plan to play primarily in co-op lobbies, prioritize team synergy and support abilities over pure individual damage numbers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best creature in Aniimo right now?

Based on current gameplay showcases, Eko is considered one of the best creatures due to its high mobility and versatile elemental attacks. However, with balancing still evolving ahead of the official September 16, 2026 launch, the top-ranked companion is subject to change.

### How does the Twine system affect combat rankings?

The Twine system lets you directly control your creature's abilities in real-time action combat, meaning mobility, attack speed, and combo potential are heavily weighted in tier rankings. Creatures with fluid animations and responsive elemental strikes generally rank higher than slow, tanky ones.

### Are starter creatures viable in the endgame?

While early-game creatures like Darkler are fantastic for exploring the starting areas of Idyll, they are currently expected to be outclassed by later-game companions. However, Pawprint Studio may implement evolution lines or Twine upgrades to keep starter creatures relevant.

### Is this Aniimo tier list finalized?

No, the tier list is not finalized. Aniimo is an unreleased game, meaning balance patches, new creatures, and unannounced mechanics will continuously shift the meta. We will update our rankings as more information and beta data become available.

### Does elemental advantage matter in Aniimo?

Yes, elemental advantages are expected to play a major role in combat effectiveness, similar to other creature-catching games. Building a balanced team with diverse elements is highly recommended to exploit enemy weaknesses during open-world encounters.
